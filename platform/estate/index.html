Direct answers about capabilities, workflow integration, and pricing.

How does Neormestate Reels generate real estate videos?
Neormestate Reels (Engine 05) accepts an MLS number or property listing URL, extracts architectural photos, highlights key amenities, and synthesizes 9:16 vertical video tours with motion graphics, captions, and licensed music in under 90 seconds.
Does Neormestate also generate the Meta lead campaign?
Yes, alongside video rendering, Neormestate writes hyper-local ad copy, selects neighborhood demographic targeting parameters, and formats instant lead generation forms for Facebook and Instagram.
Can agents white-label Neormestate videos with their own branding?
Yes, agent headshots, brokerage logos, contact details, and custom brand colors can be automatically watermarked into all video outputs and lead form banners.
How much does Neormestate Reels cost?
Neormestate Reels pricing starts at $149 per month for individual real estate agents, covering up to 10 property video packages per month.
