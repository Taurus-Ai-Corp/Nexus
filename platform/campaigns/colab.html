How It Works

1
Get Your HuggingFace Token
TRIBE v2 is a gated model on HuggingFace. Generate a free token — no payment method required. Accept the model terms at facebook/tribev2 once, then you're set.
2
Open the Notebook in Colab
Click "Open in Colab" above. The notebook connects to a free T4 GPU runtime automatically. (Runtime → Change runtime type → T4 GPU)
3
Run Cells 1-3 (One-Time Setup)
Paste your HF token, install dependencies, and download the model. Takes 3-5 minutes. The model stays cached in the Colab runtime.
4
Paste Content & Run Cells 4-7
Paste your brief, hook, or ad copy into TEXT_TO_ANALYZE. The notebook outputs full VAN/DMN/DAN/Limbic Z-scores with percentiles and downloads a JSON file.
5
Upload Results Back
On the Nexus Creative main page, click "Upload Colab Results" and select the tribev2_results.json file. Your scores are applied immediately — same as RunPod production tier but at zero cost.
