### 1.5 Bidding strategy & budget recommendations (UAE context)

**Phase 1 (First 4–6 weeks – learn & validate):**

- **B2C Search campaign:**  
  - Bid: Maximize Conversions, no tCPA at start  
  - Daily budget: ~300–400 AED/day  
- **B2B Breeder Search:**  
  - Bid: Maximize Conversions  
  - Daily budget: ~150–250 AED/day  
- **Brand/Defensive:**  
  - Bid: Maximize Clicks with bid cap (e.g. 3 AED)  
  - Daily budget: 50–100 AED/day  

Total initial Google budget: **~500–750 AED/day** (15k–22.5k AED/month).  
Once you have 50+ conversions per campaign, switch to **Target CPA**:

- B2C lead/app install target CPA: 30–70 AED  
- B2B breeder lead target CPA: 80–150 AED  

**A/B testing ideas (Google Ads):**

- Headline angle: **AI-first vs Safety-first vs Offer-first**  
- Landing page: **short vs long-form**  
- Bidding: Maximize Conversions vs Target CPA once data is available.
