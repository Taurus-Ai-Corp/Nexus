### 3.5 Cultural & Regulatory Notes

- **MOCCAE** regulates pet import/export, microchipping, rabies vaccination.
- **Dubai Municipality** enforces local pet services.
- Pets must be microchipped; rabies vaccine given after microchip, with 21-day wait before travel.
- Export health certificates valid 30 days.
- Pet care is family-friendly and culturally accepted in UAE; avoid immodest imagery.
- Islamic sensitivity: no dogs in prayer spaces imagery; focus on family/home context.
- Animal welfare messaging resonates strongly; emphasize "welfare-first" and anti-puppy-mill stance.
