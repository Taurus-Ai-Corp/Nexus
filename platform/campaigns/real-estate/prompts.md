# Neormative — Dubai Real Estate Campaign Reference

## Vertical: Dubai Real Estate
**Use case:** luxury apartments, villas, off-plan launches, short-term rentals.

## Prompt — Penthouse at Golden Hour

Ultra-realistic luxury real estate campaign photography of a modern Dubai penthouse terrace at golden hour. Wide-angle interior-exterior composition showing a sleek minimalist living room opening onto a glass balcony with panoramic views of the Burj Khalifa skyline. Warm sunlight floods the space, casting long shadows across polished marble floors. A woman in a flowing beige linen dress stands near the balcony railing, her silhouette framed against the glowing city towers. Interior features low-profile Italian furniture, soft neutral textiles, and a single sculptural vase with dried palms. Exterior skyline: Burj Khalifa, modern glass towers, warm amber sky with soft clouds. Color palette: warm beige, soft gold, deep blue shadows. Cinematic editorial color grading, architectural photography, medium-format 24mm tilt-shift lens, ultra-sharp details, natural reflections on glass and marble, HDR, premium property campaign quality, 8K resolution, luxury real estate lookbook.
