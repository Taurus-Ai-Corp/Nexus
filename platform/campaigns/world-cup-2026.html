The 4 networks that decide your campaign's fate:

VAN (Ventral Attention) — Pattern interrupt. Does your ad stop the scroll? Target: >0.5 Z-score. World Cup average VAN during matches drops 40% — attention is already high, so you need a stronger spike.

DMN (Default Mode) — Boredom. Is your ad resting the brain or engaging it? Target: negative Z-score. Every second of DMN activation is a second your audience tunes out.

DAN (Dorsal Attention) — Cognitive tracking. Is the message clear? Target: >0.3 Z-score for educational/awareness campaigns.

Limbic — Emotional resonance. Does it make people feel something? Target: >0.3 Z-score. World Cup creative with high Limbic scores outperforms by 2.4x on shareability.
