NEORM-ERA/Security

Security & Compliance

How NEORM-ERA protects customer data, workloads, and the AI orchestration layer.

Data protection

Customer content is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2+ and at rest using platform-managed keys. Access is scoped per workspace and logged.

Hiero/Hedera audit trail

Critical workflow outputs and approvals are anchored to an immutable Hiero/Hedera audit trail for tamper-evident verification.

Infrastructure

Production services run in isolated environments with least-privilege roles, automated dependency updates, and vulnerability scanning.

AI orchestration guardrails

Agent steps are gated by schema validation, human-in-the-loop approval where configured, and audit logs for every handoff.

Reporting issues

Found a vulnerability? Email us directly: admin@taurusai.io. We investigate every report.
