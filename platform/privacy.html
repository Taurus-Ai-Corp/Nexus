Privacy Policy

This policy describes how TAURUS AI CORP - FZCO collects, uses, and protects information through the NEXUS platform.

1. Information we collect

We collect contact details, workspace usage data, generated creative assets, campaign metadata, and integration tokens needed to operate Nexus Social, Creative, Intel, and Freelance.

2. How we use information

Information is used to provide the service, generate client deliverables, run analytics, enforce security, and communicate about the account.

3. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with subprocessors required to operate the platform (hosting, AI inference, authentication) under confidentiality and DPA terms.

4. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is role-based and audited. Heiro/Hedera-signed receipts create an immutable chain of custody for client deliverables.

5. Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data by emailing admin@taurusai.io.
