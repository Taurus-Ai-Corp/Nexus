## Sales Conversation Framework

### Discovery Questions
1. "What's your biggest challenge with marketing right now?"
2. "How much time does your team spend on content creation weekly?"
3. "What's working well in your current marketing approach?"
4. "What would ideal marketing automation look like for your business?"
5. "What's your current marketing budget and how is it allocated?"

### Value Proposition Delivery
"Based on what you've told me, here's how we could help:

[Specific solution based on their answers]

Most of our clients see [realistic improvement] within [timeframe] because [reason based on their situation]."

### Pricing Presentation
"Our pricing is transparent and based on the scope of work:

- If you need [basic scope], that would be our Starter package at $800/month
- For [expanded scope], our Professional package at $1,500/month would be appropriate
- The Growth package at $2,500/month includes [advanced features]

All packages include [specific deliverables] with no hidden costs or setup fees."

### Objection Handling
**"It's too expensive"**
"I understand cost is a concern. Let's look at it this way: if you're currently spending [X hours] on content creation at [hourly rate], this actually saves you money while improving quality."

**"We've tried automation before"**
"What didn't work about your previous experience? Most issues we see come from [common problems]. Our approach focuses on [our differentiator]."

**"We need to think about it"**
"That makes sense. What specific concerns do you have? I'd rather address them now than have you wonder about them later."
