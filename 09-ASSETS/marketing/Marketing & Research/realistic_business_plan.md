# TaurusAI Corp - 90-Day Business Development Plan

## Phase 1: Foundation (Days 1-30)

### Week 1: Business Infrastructure Setup
**Legal & Administrative**
- Finalize business banking for TAURUS AI Corp.
- Set up accounting system (QuickBooks or similar)
- Create basic contracts and service agreements
- Establish business insurance coverage

**Technology Setup**
- Complete Claude Code and development environment setup
- Set up professional email system
- Create project management system (Notion/Monday.com)
- Establish secure file storage and client communication tools

**Brand & Digital Presence**
- Finalize logo and brand guidelines (building on existing assets)
- Create professional LinkedIn company page
- Set up basic website: www.taurusai.io
- Establish business profiles on Google My Business

### Week 2: Service Development
**Define Core Service Packages**

**Package 1: Business Automation Starter - $800/month**
- Email marketing automation setup
- Basic chatbot implementation
- Lead capture and nurturing system
- Monthly reporting dashboard

**Package 2: Digital Marketing Pro - $1,500/month**
- Everything in Starter
- Social media content creation (12 posts/month)
- SEO optimization for 5 pages
- Landing page creation and optimization
- Bilingual content (Arabic/English or Hindi/English)

**Package 3: Growth Accelerator - $2,500/month**
- Everything in Pro
- Advanced automation workflows
- Multi-platform campaign management
- Custom dashboard development
- Weekly strategy consultations

**Create Service Documentation**
- Detailed service delivery processes
- Client onboarding checklist
- Quality control procedures
- Delivery timelines and milestones

### Week 3: Market Research and Positioning
**Competitive Analysis**
- Research 10 direct competitors in UAE/India markets
- Document their pricing, services, and positioning
- Identify specific gaps and opportunities

**Ideal Client Profiles**
- UAE: Growing SMEs with 10-50 employees, $500K-5M revenue
- India: Tech startups and agencies, $100K-2M revenue
- Canada: Professional services firms, $1M-10M revenue

**Create Market Entry Strategy**
- Identify top 20 prospects in each market
- Research their current marketing approaches
- Develop personalized outreach strategies

### Week 4: Content and Lead Generation Setup
**Content Strategy**
- Create 10 blog posts about practical AI business applications
- Develop 5 case study templates
- Design lead magnets: "AI Marketing Audit Checklist"
- Build email nurture sequence (7 emails)

**Lead Generation Infrastructure**
- Set up CRM system (HubSpot free or Pipedrive)
- Create lead scoring methodology
- Design intake forms and qualification process
- Establish referral tracking system

## Phase 2: Market Entry (Days 31-60)

### Week 5-6: Direct Outreach Campaign
**LinkedIn Strategy**
- Send 20 personalized connection requests daily
- Share 1 valuable post daily
- Engage meaningfully with prospects' content
- Schedule 5 discovery calls per week minimum

**Email Outreach**
- Send 15 personalized emails daily to qualified prospects
- Follow up sequence for non-responders
- Track open rates, response rates, and conversion metrics

**Networking and Partnerships**
- Attend 2 virtual industry events per week
- Reach out to 5 potential partnership prospects
- Join relevant business groups and communities

### Week 7-8: First Client Acquisition
**Goals for This Period**
- Secure 3 pilot clients at reduced rates
- Complete first service deliveries
- Gather testimonials and feedback
- Refine service delivery processes

**Pilot Program Structure**
- 50% discount for first 3 months
- Detailed documentation of all work performed
- Weekly check-ins and feedback sessions
- Case study development permission

## Phase 3: Scale and Optimize (Days 61-90)

**Client Success Program**
- Implement regular check-in schedules
- Create client success metrics dashboard
- Develop upselling conversation frameworks
- Establish renewal process and timeline

### Week 11-12: Growth and Expansion
**Scale Client Acquisition**
- Target 10 new qualified prospects per week

**Financial Management**
- Establish clear pricing and payment terms

- Set up financial reporting and forecasting
- Plan for team expansion and resource needs

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Monthly Metrics to Track
- New leads generated
- Conversion rate from lead to discovery call
- Conversion rate from discovery call to client
- Monthly recurring revenue (MRR)
- Client retention rate
- Average project value
- Profit margin per service line

### 90-Day Success Targets
- **Revenue**: $8,000-15,000 monthly recurring revenue
- **Clients**: 8-12 active clients across all service levels
- **Pipeline**: 20+ qualified prospects in various stages
- **Referrals**: 2-3 referrals from existing clients
- **Team**: Hire 1-2 additional team members as needed

## Risk Management and Contingency Plans

### Common Challenges and Solutions
**Cash Flow Issues**
- Require 50% payment upfront for new clients
- Offer quarterly payment discounts
- Maintain 3-month operating expense reserve

**Service Delivery Problems**
- Start with simpler services and gradually add complexity
- Over-communicate with clients about timelines
- Have backup service providers for specialized needs

**Market Competition**
- Focus on specific niches where we can excel
- Compete on value and results, not just price
- Build strong client relationships for retention

### Monthly Review Process
- Analyze all KPIs and adjust strategies accordingly
- Review client feedback and service improvements
- Assess market conditions and competitive landscape
- Plan following month's activities and goals
