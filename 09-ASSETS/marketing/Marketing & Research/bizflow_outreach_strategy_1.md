# BizFlow Outreach Strategy & Proposal Framework

## LEAD QUALIFICATION & RESEARCH STRATEGY

### Pre-Outreach Research Checklist:
- [ ] Check their current digital presence (website, social media, Google rankings)
- [ ] Identify their main competitors and their digital strategies
- [ ] Look for recent news, expansions, or business updates
- [ ] Find decision makers on LinkedIn (Marketing Manager, Business Owner, Digital Head)
- [ ] Check their current advertising (Google Ads, Facebook Ads Library)
- [ ] Analyze their website for conversion optimization opportunities

### Lead Scoring System:
**Hot Leads (Contact immediately):**
- Active on social media but poor engagement
- Website exists but not mobile-optimized
- No Google Ads presence in competitive market
- Recent business expansion or new location

**Warm Leads (Contact within week):**
- Some digital presence but inconsistent
- Good website but poor SEO rankings
- Limited social media activity

**Cold Leads (Nurture campaign):**
- Strong digital presence already
- Recently hired marketing agency
- Very small business with limited budget

## CUSTOMIZED PROPOSAL FRAMEWORK

### PROPOSAL STRUCTURE FOR ALL INDUSTRIES

#### 1. Executive Summary (1 page)
- Brief overview of their current digital challenges
- Our recommended solution approach
- Expected ROI and timeline
- Investment required

#### 2. Current Situation Analysis (1-2 pages)
- Website performance audit
- Social media presence review
- Competitor analysis
- Missed opportunities identification

#### 3. Recommended Strategy & Solutions (2-3 pages)
- Primary objectives and KPIs
- Detailed service breakdown
- Implementation timeline
- Team structure

#### 4. Expected Results & ROI (1 page)
- 3-month, 6-month, and 12-month projections
- Case studies from similar businesses
- Investment justification

#### 5. Next Steps & Investment (1 page)
- Service packages and pricing
- Implementation timeline
- What we need from them to get started

## MEETING PREPARATION & CLOSING STRATEGY

### Discovery Meeting Agenda (45 minutes):

**Minutes 1-5:** Rapport building and introductions
**Minutes 5-15:** Understanding their business and current challenges
**Minutes 15-25:** Audit presentation of their current digital presence
**Minutes 25-35:** Recommended strategy overview
**Minutes 35-40:** Success stories and case studies
**Minutes 40-45:** Next steps and proposal timeline

### Key Questions to Ask:
1. "What's your biggest challenge in attracting new customers?"
2. "How do you currently measure marketing success?"
3. "What's worked well/poorly in your past marketing efforts?"
4. "What would 'success' look like for you in 6 months?"
5. "What's your budget range for marketing investment?"

### Closing Techniques:
- **Assumption Close:** "When we start working together next month..."
- **Urgency Close:** "We only take on 3 new clients per month..."
- **Results Close:** "Based on what you've told me, I'm confident we can achieve [specific result]"

## RED FLAGS TO AVOID

### Don't Work With Companies That:
- Want guaranteed results (unrealistic expectations)
- Have no budget defined or refuse to discuss investment
- Want to manage campaigns themselves
- Have fired multiple agencies in past year
- Show no interest in long-term strategy

### Green Flags (Ideal Clients):
- Understand marketing is an investment
- Open to trying new strategies
- Responsive to communication
- Have growth mindset
- Willing to provide necessary resources/access
