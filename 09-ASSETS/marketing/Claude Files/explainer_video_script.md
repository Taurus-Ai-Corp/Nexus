## 📋 **PRODUCTION NOTES**

### Voice Talent Requirements:
- **Primary Voice:** Professional male voice with slight British accent (appeals to both markets)
- **Backup Option:** Indian-English accent for localization
- **Pace:** Energetic but clear, allowing for key points to land
- **Emotion:** Confident, authoritative, but approachable

### Music & Sound Design:
- **Music:** Upbeat, modern corporate track with Middle Eastern/Indian instruments subtly woven in
- **Sound Effects:** 
  - Cash register for revenue numbers
  - Notification sounds for social media
  - Swoosh for transitions
  - Success chimes for results

### Visual Style Guide:
- **Primary Colors:** BizFlow™ orange (#FF6B00), white, gray
- **Secondary Colors:** Dubai gold, India saffron (subtle accents)
- **Typography:** Modern, clean sans-serif (similar to Inter/Helvetica)
- **Animation Style:** Smooth, professional motion graphics with subtle 3D elements
- **Cultural Elements:** Respectful integration of Arabic patterns and Indian motifs

### Key Data Visualizations:
1. **ROI Comparison Chart** - BizFlow™ vs Industry Average
2. **Revenue Growth Curve** - Client success timeline
3. **Market Reach Map** - Dubai and India highlighted
4. **Platform Performance** - Multi-channel approach visualization

## 🔧 **TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS**

### Video Format:
- **Resolution:** 4K (3840×2160) for master, 1080p for web
- **Aspect Ratios:** 16:9 (primary), 9:16 (social), 1:1 (Instagram)
- **Frame Rate:** 30fps
- **Codec:** H.264 for web delivery

### Audio Format:
- **Sample Rate:** 48kHz
- **Bit Depth:** 24-bit
- **Format:** WAV for master, MP3 for web
- **Loudness:** -23 LUFS (broadcast standard)

### Delivery Formats:
- **Website Hero Video:** 1080p MP4, optimized for autoplay
- **YouTube/Vimeo:** 4K MP4 with embedded captions
- **Social Media:** Platform-specific optimization
- **Sales Presentations:** Uncompressed MOV file
