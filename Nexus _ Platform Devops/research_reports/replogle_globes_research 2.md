# Replogle Globes: Comprehensive Research Report

## 1. Company Overview and History

Replogle Globes is recognized as "America's Trusted Globe Manufacturer" and holds the title of the "world's largest manufacturer of globes." The company has a rich history dating back to 1930 when it was founded by Luther Replogle in Chicago, Illinois.

### Foundation and Early Years
- Founded in 1930 by Luther Replogle in Chicago
- Luther Replogle began by hand-crafting and selling globes from his apartment
- He utilized maps from England for his initial globe designs
- His vision was to make globes a common feature in both educational settings and homes

### Company Mission
The company's mission statement emphasizes "a globe in every home," founded on the belief that globes are both:
- A source of wonder
- A tool to help people make sense of their world

The company continues to be dedicated to maintaining and improving the Replogle Globes tradition of quality products and services.

## 3. Manufacturing Details

- All Replogle manufactured maps comply with US State Department's recommended guidelines (Verified Cartography)

- Features include:
  - Up-to-date cartography
  - Raised relief
  - Gyromatic assembly
