## Key Findings

1. **High Success Rate:** 8 out of 13 tested links (61%) are fully functional with proper content
2. **Strong Legal Compliance:** All legal/policy pages are complete and accessible
3. **Core Business Pages:** Essential business pages (Features, Pricing, Contact) work perfectly
4. **Resources Gap:** The entire "Resources" section contains only placeholder links
5. **Missing Support Infrastructure:** No dedicated support or community pages found

## Recommendations

### High Priority
1. **Develop Resources Section:** Create actual pages for Documentation, Learning Center, API Reference, and Community
2. **Add Support Page:** Implement a dedicated support/help center page
3. **Enterprise Page:** Develop a proper enterprise solutions page

### Medium Priority
1. **Integrations Page:** Create a page showcasing available integrations
2. **Templates Page:** Create a dedicated templates gallery (separate from homepage functionality)

### Technical Notes
- All placeholder links use `href="#"` which is standard practice for planned but unimplemented pages
- The website's core functionality and main business pages are robust and well-implemented
- Navigation structure is consistent between header and footer where applicable

## Conclusion

The Atlas AI website has a solid foundation with all essential business and legal pages functioning properly. The main area for improvement is the Resources section, which currently consists entirely of placeholder links. Developing these pages would significantly enhance the user experience and provide better support for customers and developers.
