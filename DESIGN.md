# Nexus Creative Editorial Landing Page — Design Notes

## Direction
Editorial creative-agency landing page. Not a SaaS dashboard. Not dark-mode tech.

## References
- Acne Studios lookbooks
- Vogue.com editorial pages
- High-end fashion campaign microsites
- Clean art-direction portfolios (Sonia & Co, Buero)

## Color
- Background: warm off-white `#f6f3ef`
- Paper: `#ffffff`
- Ink: `#0f0f0f`
- Muted: `#6e6e6e`
- Accent: warm clay `#b85c48`
- Secondary accent: deep indigo `#1a1a3a`

## Typography
- Display: `Syne` (Google Fonts) — geometric, fashion-editorial
- Body: `Inter` — clean, readable
- Meta/labels: `IBM Plex Mono` — small caps, technical

## Layout
- Asymmetric editorial grid
- Massive negative space
- Large image placeholders with 4:5 or 9:16 aspect ratios
- Small, confident copy
- No hero buttons in the center; CTAs sit in the footer or inline

## Sections
1. Masthead — logo + minimal nav
2. Hero statement — one large headline, one subline
3. Two campaign cards — the two prompts as case studies
4. Live prompt lab — a stripped-down input where a visitor can type a product + scene and see a generated brief
5. Process / pricing — small, at the bottom
6. Footer — contact, CTA

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- No gradient backgrounds
- No glowing buttons
- No emoji icons
- No "features" cards
- No SaaS dashboard UI
- No AI-generated-looking stock illustrations
