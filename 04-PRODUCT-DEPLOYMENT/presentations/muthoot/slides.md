# Taurus AI x Muthoot FinCorp

## AI-Powered Micro-loan Repayment Optimization

### Why This Matters Now
- Muthoot FinCorp serves millions of micro-business traders, hoteliers, grocers
- Daily/weekly micro-loan repayments cause cash-flow stress for borrowers
- High collection costs and missed payments impact portfolio health
- **Pain Point**: Borrowers struggle to align repayments with irregular cash inflows

### Our Solution: Cash-Flow AI Agent
- **AI Model**: Ollama qwen3-coder:latest (fine-tuned on synthetic repayment patterns)
- **Output**: Personalized SMS/WhatsApp reminders & repayment suggestions
- **Deployment**: Docker-compose with SQLite (zero licensing, on-prem or cloud)
- **Integration**: API or CSV exchange for borrower data & repayment status

### Value Proposition for Muthoot FinCorp
1. **Reduce Collection Costs**: Automated, timely reminders reduce manual follow-ups
2. **Improve On-time Payments**: Align reminders with borrower cash-flow patterns
3. **Enhance Borrower Experience**: Personalized, non-intrusive communication
4. **Data-Driven Insights**: Dashboard shows predicted vs actual payment rates

### Pilot Proposal
- **Target**: Head of Retail Lending (via Praveen Varkey introduction)
- **Offer**: Free pilot on 50 active micro-loans (one full repayment cycle)
- **Ask**: Access to anonymized historical data (last 3-6 months) for configuration
- **Outcome**: Testimonial + measurable improvement in on-time payments & reduced collection calls

### Expected Impact
- **>15% reduction** in collection calls
- **>10% improvement** in on-time micro-payments
- **Scalable**: $1,999/month flat OR $0.15/active loan/month after pilot
