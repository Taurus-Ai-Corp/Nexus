# Nexus Creative — Google Ads Free Account Setup Plan

## Goal
Create a free Google Ads account for Nexus Creative and launch low-budget test campaigns to drive qualified Dubai creative-agency and real-estate prospects.

## Step 1: Create the account (no payment required)
1. Go to https://ads.google.com
2. Sign in with admin@taurusai.io (or create a new Google Ads manager account)
3. Choose **Switch to Expert Mode** (bottom-left link on the setup page)
4. Select **Create an account without a campaign**
5. Confirm country: United Arab Emirates
6. Currency: AED
7. Time zone: Dubai (GMT+4)

## Step 2: Account structure
- Account name: Nexus Creative by Taurus AI
- Campaign type: Search
- Bidding: Maximize conversions (start with manual CPC if budget is tiny)
- Daily budget: AED 30–50 (~$8–14/day) per campaign

## Step 4: Conversion tracking
- Add a Google tag to both landing pages.
- Track:
  - Page view
  - CTA click (mailto + try demo)
  - Prompt lab interaction (button click)
- No purchase tracking yet; optimize for leads/engagement.

## Step 5: Billing
- Add a payment method only when ready to spend.
- Start with AED 90–150/day total across all campaigns.
- Pause any campaign with CTR below 1% after 3 days.

## Step 6: Optimization checklist
- [ ] Search terms report daily
- [ ] Add negative keywords: free, job, internship, software engineer
- [ ] A/B test two headline variants per campaign
- [ ] Increase budget on campaigns with CTR > 3% and demo clicks
