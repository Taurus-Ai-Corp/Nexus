# Nexus Creative — Style Training Reference

## Purpose
Use these prompts as the canonical "luxury editorial" and "sporty-luxury summer" reference styles for Nexus Creative demos and outbound creative samples.

---

## Prompt A — Nordic Tech-Street Editorial

**Use case:** Streetwear / luxury basics / fashion lookbook / brand hero shots.

**Full prompt:**
Ultra-realistic high-fashion editorial studio portrait of a young woman standing centered in frame, full-body view, facing directly toward the camera with a calm, serious, slightly intimidating expression and intense eye contact. Long silver-blonde ash hair with darker roots, naturally wavy and slightly tousled, middle-parted, falling past the shoulders. Pale skin, sharp facial structure, subtle natural makeup, realistic skin texture, light-colored eyes. She wears an oversized black technical streetwear t-shirt with subtle white piping accents and a small futuristic white logo on the chest, paired with loose black cargo pants. Accessories include multiple silver rings on both hands, a thin silver chain necklace with a cross pendant, and a delicate silver bracelet. Both hands are casually tucked into her pockets. Relaxed posture, straight stance, minimalist styling. Shot in a seamless white-to-light-gray studio environment with a clean gradient background and large negative space above the subject. Powerful diffused backlight creates a luminous halo around the hair and shoulders, combined with soft frontal fill lighting that preserves facial details while maintaining gentle shadows. Cool monochromatic color grading, subtle contrast, cinematic mood, Scandinavian minimalism, luxury streetwear campaign aesthetic. Professional fashion photography, medium-format camera, 85mm lens, eye-level angle, symmetrical composition, full-body framing, vertical portrait orientation (9:16), ultra-sharp focus on eyes, detailed hair strands, realistic fabric folds and textures, natural skin pores, HDR, premium editorial retouching, luxury magazine cover quality, photorealistic, hyperrealistic, 8K resolution, fashion lookbook, contemporary cyber-minimalist aesthetic.

**Style tags to reuse:**
- Nordic minimalism, clean gradient studio, cyber-minimalist
- Silver-blonde / ash hair, pale skin, natural texture
- Black technical streetwear, white piping, futuristic micro-logo
- Diffused backlight + soft frontal fill
- Cool monochromatic grade, medium-format 85mm
- 9:16 vertical portrait, full-body centered, negative space

## Prompt B — Tropical Sport-Luxury Sunglasses

**Use case:** Eyewear / swimwear / activewear / summer luxury campaigns.

**Full prompt:**
Ultra-realistic luxury summer sunglasses campaign photography of a young athletic woman on a tropical beach volleyball court under intense midday sunlight. She has short messy wavy brunette hair blowing naturally in the wind, glowing tan skin with glossy sunlit highlights, soft natural makeup, full lips slightly parted, confident sporty expression. She wears futuristic oversized wraparound shield sunglasses with a single-piece reflective orange-to-red mirrored visor lens and a thick white aerodynamic sporty frame. Outfit consists of a fitted white ribbed sleeveless crop tank top and white high-waisted shorts styled with a slim brown leather belt. Camera positioned extremely close and low to the subject using a wide-angle fashion lens, creating a dynamic perspective with the model leaning toward the camera in an energetic athletic stance. Upper body dominates the frame while arms extend outward naturally, giving motion and intensity. Sunglasses are the hero focus with strong glossy reflections of the bright beach environment visible across the mirrored visor lens. Background features soft blurred tropical beach scenery with bright cyan-blue sky tones, white beach architecture, subtle volleyball court atmosphere, and dreamy shallow depth of field. Harsh tropical sunlight creates bright specular highlights across skin, shoulders, collarbones, and sunglasses while maintaining soft cinematic shadows. Warm golden-orange skin tones contrast against cool cyan and turquoise beach hues for a premium luxury summer aesthetic. Hyperrealistic skin texture with visible pores and natural sheen, cinematic editorial color grading inspired by Vogue and high-end luxury sportswear campaigns, ultra-detailed fabric texture, realistic wind movement, reflective lens flares, premium sporty-luxury mood, fashion commercial photography, shallow DOF, 8k ultra realism, cinematic composition, luxury eyewear advertisement aesthetic.

**Style tags to reuse:**
- Tropical sport-luxury, beach volleyball setting, midday sun
- Orange-to-red mirrored visor sunglasses, white aerodynamic frame
- Warm golden-orange skin vs cyan-turquoise background
- Low wide-angle fashion lens, dynamic lean
- Shallow DOF, glossy reflections, lens flares
- Vogue / luxury sportswear campaign color grade

## How to use in Nexus Creative
1. Store these as `creative-styles/nordic-tech-street.md` and `creative-styles/tropical-sport-luxury.md`.
2. When a prospect picks a vibe, prepend the matching style block to their product prompt.
3. Use the generated images in portfolio landing pages, ad mockups, and outbound creative samples.
4. Extract reusable tokens (lighting, lens, grade, pose) into a shared `style-dictionary.md`.

---

## Voice for the landing page
- Confident, sparse, editorial.
- No buzzwords: "AI-powered", "revolutionize", "unlock", "leverage".
- Headlines should read like a fashion house: "The campaign brief. Solved.", "Less deck. More image.", "Made for brands that move fast."
