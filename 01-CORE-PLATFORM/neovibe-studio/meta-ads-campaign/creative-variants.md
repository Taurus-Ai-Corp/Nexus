# NeoVibe — Creative Variants (5 ads)

**Voice rules:**
- No "revolutionary", "game-changer", "unleash". Designers will block-report.
- Use specific verbs: ship, export, approve, reuse, query.
- Reference real tools by name (Figma, Webflow, Slack) — not "your design tool".
- Mention concrete time savings, not vague productivity.
- Self-deprecating > earnest. Acknowledge the chaos before pitching the fix.

---

## Variant 1 — "Stop Stitching 7 Tools"

- **Hook (headline)**: Stop stitching 7 tools.
- **Primary text** (~70 words):
  > Figma for design. ChatGPT for copy. Midjourney for hero images. Notion for the brief. Slack for the brand kit nobody can find. Loom for the handoff. Drive for everything else.
  >
  > NeoVibe is one workspace. AI image gen + video gen + a playground that exports clean Tailwind code. Your brand kit lives where you design. Application-only — we keep it small.
  >
  > Apply for early access in Kerala and the UAE.
- **CTA**: Apply Now
- **Visual**: Split-screen. Left: 7 chaotic browser tabs (labels visible: Figma, ChatGPT, Midjourney, Notion, Slack, Drive, Loom). Right: one calm NeoVibe workspace with the same project. Subtle motion — left side flickers/refreshes, right side breathes.
- **Why it works**: Names the pain by tool name. Designers immediately recognize their own tab graveyard. "Application-only" reframes the gate as exclusivity, not friction.

## Variant 2 — "AI That Actually Ships Code"

- **Hook**: AI that actually ships code.
- **Primary text** (~65 words):
  > Most "AI design tools" stop at the mockup. NeoVibe's playground exports production-ready Next.js + Tailwind components — typed, themeable, ready to ship.
  >
  > 90 seconds from brief to a component you can drop into a client repo.
  >
  > For freelancers and 2-5 person studios who'd rather build than babysit prompts. Apply for access — we approve in 48 hours.
- **CTA**: Get Access
- **Visual**: Screen recording. AI-generated hero design appears on the playground canvas, then morphs/animates into clean Tailwind JSX code on the right side. Final frame: the JSX pasted into a `components/Hero.tsx` file in VSCode. Caption: "90s · brief → component."
- **Why it works**: The "AI = mockups only" objection is the #1 reason designers ignore AI tools. Leading with "ships code" is a wedge against Figma + Midjourney + manual rebuild.

## Variant 4 — "From Brief to Component in Minutes"

- **Hook**: Brief in. Component out.
- **Primary text** (~60 words):
  > Drop the client brief in. The RAG knows your past assets, your brand kit, your common patterns.
  >
  > Generate hero, generate icons, lay it out in the playground, export Next.js + Tailwind. Send the link. Get paid Tuesday.
  >
  > Beta access for designers in Kerala and UAE. Approval in 48 hours.
- **CTA**: Sign Up
- **Visual**: 3-frame storyboard, single image. Frame 1: a PDF brief icon labeled "client_brief.pdf". Arrow. Frame 2: NeoVibe playground with hero + components rendered. Arrow. Frame 3: a clean code file. Whole thing readable in 1.5 seconds.
- **Why it works**: Concrete workflow narrative. "Get paid Tuesday" is the right kind of self-deprecating — no designer is romantic about their work, they're romantic about getting paid. RAG is mentioned but not jargonized.

## Variant 5 — "Built by Freelancers for Freelancers"

- **Hook**: We built it because we were tired.
- **Primary text** (~70 words):
  > We're a Kerala-and-Dubai design team. We built NeoVibe because Figma + ChatGPT + Midjourney + Notion + Drive made us slower, not faster.
  >
  > One workspace. AI gen baked in. Code export. RAG over your asset library. No Discord required.
  >
  > Application-only beta. We approve in 48 hours, or tell you why not. Free for the first 200 designers in Kerala and the UAE.
- **CTA**: Apply Now
- **Visual**: Real (not stock) photo — a designer at a coffee shop, NeoVibe playground on screen, a notebook with sketches next to the laptop. Slightly grainy, slightly imperfect. NOT a polished render.
- **Why it works**: Founder/peer voice beats brand voice for this audience. Geographic specificity ("Kerala-and-Dubai") signals the campaign isn't dropshipped from California. "Or tell you why not" addresses the curation gate honestly — most rejection-based products hide rejection; saying it out loud disarms it.

## Placement Strategy

- **Auto-placements** for week 1 — let the algo find efficient surfaces.
- Expect Reels + IG Feed to dominate for variants 1, 2, 4 (motion-heavy).
- Variants 3 and 5 will likely lean Feed + Stories (static-friendly).
- After week 1, manually exclude Audience Network (poor signal for B2B SaaS) and Right Column (not a designer surface).

## Aspect Ratios to Produce per Variant

- 1:1 (Feed) — required
- 9:16 (Reels + Stories) — required for variants 1, 2, 4
- 4:5 (Feed mobile) — optional but lifts CTR ~10-15% on Feed

## Localization

- **Kerala set**: English copy default. Test Malayalam version of Variant 5 only — peer/founder angle benefits most from native language. Don't translate the others; designers in Kerala read English design content natively.
- **UAE set**: English only. UAE designer market is English-first regardless of nationality.
