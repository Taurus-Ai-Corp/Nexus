Wellness & Spiritual Life

Body, Mind, and Spirit

At Mater Maria, wellness is not a programme — it is a way of life. We nurture the whole person through holistic care that honours the body, engages the mind, and nourishes the spirit.

Holistic Wellness

Three Pillars of Care

Our wellness philosophy integrates Kerala's ancient healing traditions with modern medical care and the spiritual richness of our faith community.

Ayurvedic Spa

Our in-house Ayurvedic spa offers traditional Kerala therapies — from Abhyanga oil massage and Shirodhara to Njavarakizhi. Administered by certified practitioners, these treatments promote circulation, ease joint pain, and restore vitality. Steam suites and herbal baths complement every session.

Wellness Cafe

The Wellness Cafe serves nutrient-rich meals crafted by our in-house nutritionist. From traditional Kerala sadya to heart-healthy continental options, every dish is prepared with farm-fresh organic produce. Dietary needs — diabetic, low-sodium, gluten-free — are honoured with care.

Recovery Programmes

Personalised recovery packages for post-surgical care, physiotherapy rehabilitation, and chronic pain management. Our programmes integrate modern physiotherapy with yoga therapy and garden-based occupational activities to accelerate healing in a nurturing environment.

Mater Maria Chapel
A Community of Faith

Spiritual Life

Rooted in the Syro-Malabar Catholic tradition and open to all who seek spiritual nourishment, our chapel and faith programmes provide comfort, connection, and purpose.

Daily Chapel Services

Morning and evening prayer services in our on-campus chapel, with Holy Mass celebrated weekly. Special liturgies for feast days and the liturgical calendar bring the community together in worship.

Prayer Groups

Small-group Bible study, Rosary circles, and intercessory prayer gatherings that foster deep friendships and shared spiritual growth among residents. Ecumenical groups welcome Christians of all traditions.

Spiritual Counselling

Our resident chaplain is available for personal counselling, sacramental preparation, and pastoral care. Grief support, life transition guidance, and companionship for those in need of spiritual comfort.

Quarterly Retreats

Seasonal spiritual retreats led by visiting clergy and religious leaders — offering days of reflection, renewal, and deeper engagement with faith. Past themes have included gratitude, forgiveness, and finding grace in aging.

Healing Through Nature

Nature Therapy

Our campus is a living ecosystem — a place where residents reconnect with the earth, find peace in green spaces, and experience the therapeutic power of nature.

Organic Gardens

Resident-tended vegetable and herb gardens supplying fresh produce to our kitchen daily.

Fish Farm

A backyard aquaculture pond where residents can enjoy leisure fishing and engage with sustainable food production.

Fruit Orchards

Mango, jackfruit, coconut, and papaya trees shade the walking paths and provide seasonal harvests.

Walking Paths

Shaded, barrier-free walking trails winding through landscaped gardens with seating at regular intervals.
