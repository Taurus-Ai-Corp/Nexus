# Business Documents Folder

## Purpose
This folder contains all legal, financial, and official business documents related to the Mater Maria Homes project.

## Contents
- **Contracts** - Legal agreements with clients, vendors, and partners
- **Invoices** - Billing documents for services rendered and payments received
- **Agreements** - Service agreements, NDAs, and partnership contracts
- **Certificates** - Completion certificates, licenses, and regulatory approvals
- **Correspondence** - Official letters, emails, and communication records
- **Financial Records** - Budget documents, expense reports, and financial statements

## Substructure
```
business-documents/
├── contracts/                   # Legal agreements and contracts
├── invoices/                    # Billing and payment documents
├── agreements/                  # Service agreements and NDAs
├── certificates/                # Licenses, certifications, and approvals
├── correspondence/              # Official letters and communication
├── financial/                   # Budget, expenses, and financial reports
└── pdf/                         # PDF versions of all documents (for easy sharing)
```

## Document Types Found
- Service Agreements
- Invoices (Phase-based)
- Project Completion Certificates
- Change Request Templates
- Brutal Honest Assessments (internal reviews)
- NDA/Confidentiality Agreements
- Board Meeting Agendas
- Executive Speaking Notes
- Upsell Strategy Documents

## Naming Convention
Use descriptive names with dates and version numbers:
- `01-INVOICE-PHASE1-[date].pdf`
- `02-SERVICE-AGREEMENT-[client-name]-[date].pdf`
- `03-PROJECT-COMPLETION-[project-name]-[date].pdf`
- `nda-[party-name]-[date].pdf`

## Related Folders
- **`devops/`** - Operational procedures that reference these contracts
- **`strategic_documents/`** - High-level business strategy and planning
- **`inputs/`** - Source data that may inform contractual terms
- **`quality_assurance/`** - Compliance validation against contractual requirements
