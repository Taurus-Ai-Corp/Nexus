1. Purpose

Both parties ("Disclosing Party" and "Receiving Party") wish to explore and engage in a business relationship involving website development and digital marketing services. During this relationship, both parties may share confidential information that must be protected.

2. Definition of Confidential Information

Confidential Information includes but is not limited to:

  • Financial models, investment structures, pricing strategies, and shareholder information
  • Investor lead data, contact information, and inquiry details captured through the website
  • Board member personal information, credentials, and communications
  • Property specifications, floor plans, and architectural drawings
  • Business strategies, marketing plans, and client acquisition methods
  • Source code, design files, API keys, and technical infrastructure details
  • AI/LLM prompts, workflows, and proprietary development methodologies

3. Obligations

3.1 The Receiving Party shall not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent.

3.2 The Receiving Party shall use Confidential Information solely for the purposes of the business relationship.

3.3 The Receiving Party shall protect Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care used to protect its own confidential information.

4. Exclusions

This NDA does not apply to information that: (a) is publicly available; (b) was known prior to disclosure; (c) is independently developed; (d) is required by law or court order to be disclosed.

5. Data Protection

5.1 The Provider shall comply with applicable data protection laws including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 regarding any personal data processed through the website.

5.2 Investor lead data captured through the website is owned by the Client. The Provider shall not use this data for any purpose other than delivering the contracted services.

5.3 The Provider shall implement reasonable technical measures to protect data in transit and at rest.

6. Term

This NDA is effective from the date of signing and remains in force for two (2) years after the termination of the business relationship.

7. Return of Information

Upon termination, each party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information in its possession within 30 days, and certify such return or destruction in writing.

8. Governing Law

This NDA is governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates.
