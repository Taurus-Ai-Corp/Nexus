## ITEM 6: Board Feedback — Your Role

**You are LISTENING, not defending.**

When feedback comes:
- Nod and write it down
- Say: "Thank you, that's noted. We'll address that in the refinement phase."
- Do NOT explain why something is the way it is (unless directly asked)
- Do NOT promise changes on the spot
- Do NOT give timelines for individual changes

**If someone asks "can we change X?":**
> "Absolutely — we can accommodate that. I'll include it in our next update cycle."

**If someone says "I don't like the color/font/layout":**
> "I appreciate that feedback. Could you describe what feeling you'd prefer? Warmer? More traditional? That helps me get it right."

**If someone's nephew "knows computers":**
> [smile] "That's great. Happy to share the technical documentation with anyone on the team who'd like to review it."

**If the Bishop speaks — whatever he says, agree first:**
> "That's an excellent point, Your Grace. We'll make sure that's reflected."
