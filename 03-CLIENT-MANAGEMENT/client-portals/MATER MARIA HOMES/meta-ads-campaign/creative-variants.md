# Creative Variants — Five Angles

Tone discipline: thoughtful advisor, not hawker. No "God's Own Country", no "Kerala's pride", no all-caps urgency, no emoji barrages. Each variant uses at most one tasteful emoji or none. Copy speaks to a 50-year-old chief accountant in Sharjah who has read prospectuses before and will close the tab the moment something feels promotional.

> **Compliance note (read before editing any variant):** Every variant below carries two mandatory footers — (1) the SEBI standard warning verbatim and (2) the consolidated cross-jurisdiction disclaimer with a `[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]` placeholder. Both are launch-blocking under SEBI's advertisement code and the UAE CMA framework effective 2026-01-01. The placeholder must be filled by counsel before submission to Meta — do not ship with `[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]` unresolved. See `meta-ads-campaign/policy-compliance.md § 2`.

## Notes for Production

- All five copy variants will run as 1:1 (Feed) and 4:5 (Feed-tall) static at minimum. Reels (9:16) versions added in week 2 once we identify the top two static performers.
- Headlines (the 27-character field below the primary text) should mirror the hook, not repeat it. Examples:
  - V1: *Reserve from ₹5 lakhs*
  - V2: *Run the calculator*
  - V3: *Patron: Bp. Pulickal*
  - V4: *24/7 on-site nursing*
  - V5: *90 residences. 4 tiers.*
- Description (the 27-character optional field): *matermariahomes.com/invest* across all variants.
- **Legal disclaimer placement (mandatory, every creative):** Both footer blocks pasted under each variant above must appear on the rendered ad — the SEBI standard warning at minimum 10pt (or visual equivalent on-screen) inside the image/video frame, and the consolidated ad-copy disclaimer in the primary-text footer area visible without "see more" expansion. The `[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]` placeholder must be replaced by counsel with the issuing entity's full registered name before any creative is uploaded to Meta.
- **No promise / superlative language** anywhere in headline, primary text, image overlay, or thumbnail copy. Banned terms include "best", "highest", "guaranteed", "risk-free", "the only", "unmatched", "ultimate", and any synonym implying certainty of return. SEBI's 2023+ enforcement record makes these per-se violations.
