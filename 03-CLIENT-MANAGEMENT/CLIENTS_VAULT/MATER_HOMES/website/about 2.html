About Us

Our Story

Founded in faith and rooted in the Christian values of Kerala's rich tradition, Mater Maria is a community built on love, service, and the belief that every season of life deserves dignity.

Our Heritage

Founded in Faith

Mater Maria Wellness Homes is deeply rooted in the Christian values that have shaped Kerala's Syro-Malabar Catholic tradition for centuries. The name "Mater Maria" — Latin for "Mother Mary" — reflects our guiding inspiration: the unconditional love, nurturing spirit, and quiet strength that defined the life of the Blessed Virgin.

In Kottayam, where the Church has been a pillar of education, healthcare, and social service for generations, we saw an opportunity to extend that legacy into the realm of senior living. Our founders envisioned a place where the elderly would not merely reside, but truly belong — surrounded by faith, nature, and a community that honours their wisdom and worth.

Every aspect of Mater Maria — from the morning prayers in our chapel to the organic garden tended by residents, from the Ayurvedic wellness centre to the shared meals in our community dining hall — is designed to nurture the whole person: body, mind, and spirit. We are not simply building homes; we are cultivating a community of grace.

What Drives Us

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

A Community of Dignity

To create retirement communities inspired by the values of the Church — dignity, compassion, and stewardship — where seniors and families can find belonging, wellness, and purpose in a sustainable and caring environment.

Our Vision

A Legacy of Grace

To redefine retirement living in Kerala by creating a values-driven, sustainable community where dignity, wellness, and belonging come first. We envision a future where faith-inspired care, ecological responsibility, and social good come together to create a legacy of grace for generations.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Six foundational principles guide every decision we make, every space we build, and every life we touch.

Dignity

Every resident is treated with the respect and honour they deserve. We believe that growing older is not a diminishment but a deepening — of wisdom, of faith, and of grace.

Compassion

Inspired by the boundless compassion of Christ, our care goes beyond physical needs. We attend to the emotional, social, and spiritual well-being of every resident.

Stewardship

We are custodians of the trust families place in us. Every resource, every rupee, every hour of care is managed with accountability, transparency, and a sense of sacred duty.

Community

Human beings thrive in connection. We foster meaningful relationships among residents, families, and staff — creating a vibrant community where no one walks alone.

Sustainability

As stewards of God's creation, we are committed to ecological responsibility — from energy-efficient buildings and rainwater harvesting to organic gardens and waste-conscious operations.

Faith

Faith is the foundation upon which Mater Maria stands. We honour the Christian tradition while welcoming all who share our values of love, service, and reverence for life.

Our Journey

Our Legacy

A timeline of milestones marking the growth of a dream into a living, breathing community.

2024

Founded in Purpose

Mater Maria Wellness Homes Pvt Ltd was incorporated in Kottayam by a group of professionals, clergy, and social workers united by a shared conviction: that Kerala's elders deserve retirement living that honours their faith, their culture, and their dignity. The founding charter was signed at a ceremony blessed by the local diocese.

2025

First Groundbreaking

Construction began on our flagship community along Puthuppally Road, Kottayam. Designed by award-winning architects with deep expertise in tropical sustainable design, the campus integrates Kerala's traditional architectural language — courtyards, verandahs, sloped roofs — with modern accessibility and wellness infrastructure.

2026

Community Launch

Mater Maria opens its doors to its first residents. The community features 120 residences across three tiers, a full-service wellness centre, chapel, community dining, landscaped gardens, and round-the-clock healthcare support. A new chapter in Kerala's retirement living begins.
