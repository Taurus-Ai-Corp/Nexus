A credit is used when you download a generated ad creative. Each plan includes a set number of monthly credits, which renew every month. You can generate unlimited creatives, but credits are only consumed when you download them.

Brands are the foundation of your creatives on AdCreative.ai. By creating a brand, you can upload your logo, brand colors, brand descriptions, and connect your ad accounts. This allows our machine-learning model to tailor your creative designs and predictions to your brand, ensuring the highest quality output.

With AdCreative.ai, you have the freedom to generate as many creatives as you want, regardless of whether you have used all your downloads or not. You will only use your downloads when you choose to download your generated creatives.

With AdCreative.ai, you have access to over 100 million free stock images to use in your ad creatives. These images are included with every package, and you will not be charged any additional fees for their use.

Our Text Generator AI feature allows you to generate high-converting ad texts and headlines using a variety of copywriting methodologies. This feature is included in every package at no additional cost.

By connecting your ad accounts, our AI can analyze your creatives and provide you with insights that you won’t find anywhere else. These insights can include your average CTR in your brand category, your best-performing colors and creatives, and much more.

AdCreative.ai believes that teamwork makes the dream work. That’s why we allow you to invite users to your account, collaborate on projects, and work together seamlessly to achieve your creative goals.

We offer refunds within 7 days for monthly plans and 30 days for yearly plans, provided the platform hasn’t been used (e.g., generating creatives, downloading assets). Eligible refunds are typically processed the same day, though it may take up to 1–2 weeks to appear in your account depending on your bank. You can learn more in our [Terms and Conditions](https://adcreative.ai/legal/en/terms-of-service-for-online-subscribers).

Integrations allow you to connect your ad accounts to your brands on AdCreative.ai. This helps to fine-tune our machine-learning model for you, ensuring that the creative designs and predictions you see are specifically tailored to your brand.
