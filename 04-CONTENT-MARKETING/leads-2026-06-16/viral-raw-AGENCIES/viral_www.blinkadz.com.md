## Expand your company with Blinkadz

*   ### Real Estate Agents: Personalized Property Tours

Problem

Real estate agents need to create unique property showcase videos for hundreds of listings, but manual video creation is too time-consuming and expensive. Solution

Upload a CSV with property details (address, price, features, agent name), map variables to a template, and generate personalized video tours for your entire portfolio. Each video automatically includes property photos, listing details, and agent contact info.   
![Image 12: Real estate property showcase](https://www.blinkadz.com/assets/home/expand/1.svg) 
*   ### Sales Teams: Account-Based Video Outreach

Problem

Sales teams need to send personalized video messages to thousands of prospects, but creating individual videos manually is impossible at scale. Solution

Export your CRM data to CSV (prospect name, company, pain point, solution), choose a sales outreach template, and generate personalized pitch videos for each prospect. Dramatically increase response rates with hyper-personalized video outreach at scale.   
![Image 13: Sales team collaborating on outreach strategy](https://www.blinkadz.com/assets/home/expand/2.svg) 
*   ### Marketing Agencies: Multi-Client Campaign Videos

Problem

Marketing agencies need to produce hundreds of customized video ads across multiple clients and campaigns, which requires extensive resources. Solution

Create a CSV with campaign data for all clients (brand name, offer, CTA, logo, colors), select templates, and generate thousands of brand-specific videos in one batch. Serve more clients efficiently while maintaining quality and personalization.   
![Image 14: Marketing team analyzing campaign performance](https://www.blinkadz.com/assets/home/expand/3.svg) 
*   ### E-commerce: Product-Specific Video Ads

Problem

E-commerce businesses need unique video ads for hundreds or thousands of products, but creating individual product videos is prohibitively expensive. Solution

Export your product catalog as CSV (product name, image, price, features, discount), map to a product showcase template, and generate professional video ads for every product. Perfect for seasonal campaigns, flash sales, and new product launches.
