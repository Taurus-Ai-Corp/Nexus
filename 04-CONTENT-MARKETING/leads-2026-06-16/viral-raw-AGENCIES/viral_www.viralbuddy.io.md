Not at all. Viralbuddy is designed for non-writers, busy professionals, founders, and marketers. It adapts to your tone and goals so your content sounds like _you_, just better.

[3. What types of content can I create with Viralbuddy?](https://www.viralbuddy.io/#expand-f0922e6)

You can use it for both! Many users create posts for founders, executives, and company brand accounts.

[5. How is Viralbuddy different from ChatGPT or other AI tools?](https://www.viralbuddy.io/#expand-bcc1439)

Viralbuddy is built specifically for LinkedIn. It offers pre-tuned prompts, real-time trends, engagement-tested formats, and tools like video-to-post and carousel generators. All tailored to LinkedIn growth.
