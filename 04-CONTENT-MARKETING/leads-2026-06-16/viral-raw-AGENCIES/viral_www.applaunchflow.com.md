## Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about AppLaunchFlow.

How can I create screenshots, promo videos, and ASO copy in one tool?

AppLaunchFlow is an all-in-one app launch asset workspace: one project holds your App Store screenshots, promo videos, ASO copy, localization, app icons, 3D mockups, social graphics, and keyword tracking — instead of stitching together Figma, Canva, a video tool, a translation service, and an ASO spreadsheet. It is not a CI/CD pipeline or app builder; it covers store creatives, metadata, and App Store Connect handoff.

What is the best all-in-one tool for launching a mobile app?

For launch assets, AppLaunchFlow covers the full set: AI-generated App Store and Play Store screenshots, preview videos, ASO metadata, localized variants, icons, mockups, and social graphics in one workspace, with keyword tracking to measure the results. App builders and CI/CD tools handle shipping the binary; AppLaunchFlow handles everything the store listing needs.

How do I manage all app launch assets in one workspace?

Upload your raw app screenshots once. AppLaunchFlow reuses them — plus your brand colors and copy — across screenshots, promo videos, social graphics, and mockups, so every asset stays consistent and updates propagate from one source instead of five disconnected tools.

How do I get from raw screenshots to store-ready assets for iOS and Google Play?

Import raw captures, pick a template, and export complete sets in every required App Store Connect and Google Play size — then hand off directly via store connections. Apple's and Google's size requirements are built into the export presets.

Is AppLaunchFlow really free to use?

You can try AppLaunchFlow for free to explore the editor and generate copy. Full exports are included in the single paid plan, and there’s also a one-time export option for promo videos and screenshots.

Can I export screenshots for both iOS and Android?

Yes. AppLaunchFlow supports all required App Store and Google Play screenshot sizes. You can export phone and tablet screenshots optimized for iOS, Android, and iPad in the correct dimensions.

Do I need design experience to create App Store screenshots?

No. AppLaunchFlow is built for developers and product teams. You choose a template, upload your app screenshots, and edit everything in a simple visual editor—no Figma or design skills required.

How does AppLaunchFlow generate screenshots and copy?

AppLaunchFlow analyzes your uploaded screenshots to understand layout, colors, and content. It then helps generate optimized screenshot layouts, headlines, and App Store copy that match your app and category.

Can I translate my App Store screenshots into other languages?

Yes. The plan includes translations for your App Store screenshots and descriptions into multiple languages. Layouts automatically adjust for different text lengths so your screenshots stay clean and readable.

Can I use the generated screenshots and illustrations commercially?

Yes. You fully own the screenshots, illustrations, and copy you generate with AppLaunchFlow and can use them commercially for your App Store and Play Store listings.

Do you offer a one-time export option?
