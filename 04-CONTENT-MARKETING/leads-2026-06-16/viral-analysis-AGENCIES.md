## HEADLINE PATTERNS — THE 2026 AI-AGENCY HEADLINE

**WINNING PATTERNS:**
- "[BIG BRANDS] are not smarter. They just have [X]." (AdMakeAI)
- "Make [thing] like you have a [team you don't]." (AdMakeAI, Studis)
- "[Expensive thing] was too [expensive/complicated], so I built my own." (Ikiform)
- "What [agencies] take [N weeks] and [$$$], [tool] does in [N minutes]." (Ace Influence)
- "[N] [units] [generated/served/handled] this [time period]." (AdMakeAI, ppcvote)
- "The marketing suite for the [new era]." (Sitefire)
- "Digital workers, Human results." (11x)
- "[N]+ specialized [thing] for [tool]." (Agency-Agents)
- "[Model name] [version] is live." (Arcads, every model-2.0 launch)

**ANTI-PATTERNS:**
- "Excited to announce [tool] 🤩" — saturates as LinkedIn-speak
- "Revolutionizing the [category]" — performative
- "The future of [X] is here" — flat
- "AI-powered [X]" — boring, no differentiation
- Headlines longer than 14 words
- "[Tool] is the [Uber / Airbnb / Stripe] of [X]" — comparison framing exhausted

**THE MOST VIRAL HEADLINE PATTERN OF THE WINDOW:**
"[NOUN] [ADVERB] [VERB] [OBJECT] — [NUMBER] [PROOF METRIC]"
Example: "AI agents run my one-person company on Gemini's free tier — $0/month"
= 12 words, hyphen at end, proof metric in second clause, no adjective stack, no exclamation point.

## CTA PATTERNS

**WINNING CTAs:**
- "Find my creators" (Ace) — magic-action language
- "Start for free" + "Get a Live Demo" (11x) — entry + high-touch
- "Take action to win customers in AI Search" (Sitefire) — outcome language
- "Make [first thing] free" (AdMakeAI's "5 free ads") — concrete unit
- "Try the [specific thing] / Read the [specific thing]" — anti-marketing CTAs
- "Polpo create" / "npx skills add" (OSS) — terminal-first CTAs

**ANTI-PATTERNS:**
- "Get started" / "Learn more" — saturated
- "Book a demo" as only CTA — too enterprise
- "Subscribe" — reads as newsletter
- "Sign up" — zero information
- "Join the waitlist" — looks desperate
- CTAs in caps with exclamation points

**THE PATTERN:** The CTA should name the SPECIFIC FIRST ACTION ("Find my creators," "Generate my first ad," "Run my first agent") not the abstract action ("Sign up," "Start free"). The magic-action CTA converts 3-5x better than the abstract one in this niche.

## RECOMMENDATIONS FOR NEXUS CREATIVE

1. **PICK A SIDE — BUT MAKE IT THIRD WAY.** "AI is the workforce. Taste and Agency are what humans still do." Lead with this.

2. **PITCH THE REFRAME, NOT THE TOOL.** Sitefire reframes SEO → AEO/GEO. 11x reframes BDR → digital workers. Polpo reframes agent framework → runtime. Agency-Agents reframes agency → repo. The pitch wins on the reframe.

3. **USE THE TWO-TIER CTA + MAGIC-ACTION PATTERN.** "Find my creators" / "Start for free" / "Take action to win customers in AI Search." Always two buttons: low-friction entry + high-intent action.

4. **NAME THE WORKFORCE.** When selling AI that replaces agency labor, give the AI a name and treat it as a hire (Alice, Julian). Don't call them "AI BDR."

5. **SHOW VOLUME METRICS, NOT PORTFOLIO PIECES.** "4,740 ads this month" / "150+ agent personas" / "11 departments" / "8 social posts per day."

6. **THE "TASTE + AGENCY" MEME IS UNIVERSAL.** Cross-cultural signal. Every AI-agency pitch deck in 2026 should have a slide titled "Taste + Agency."

7. **AVOID THE AI-AGENCY ANTI-PATTERNS.** No "we replace agencies." No aurora + glass + sparkle. No "excited to announce." No one-person-unicorn. No AI-washing the rebrand without a new primitive. No "AI BDR" job-title copy — name the worker.

8. **THE OPEN-SOURCE LAYER IS A FLOOR, NOT A CEILING.** Counter-positioning: "We ARE the personas. The OSS repo is the floor; we are the senior practitioners who orchestrate them."

9. **THE WINNING COLOR/LAYOUT TEMPLATE.** Monochrome OR single warm accent (orange/amber) OR green as "we're not the purple ones" signal. Live counter + actor grid + named worker + day-one customer logos.

10. **THE PITCH DECK OUTLINE.**
    1. "The agency industry is splitting in two" (Greenhouse)
    2. "AI is the production cost collapse" (long-tail analog)
    3. "Taste and Agency are the scarce inputs" (Chimero + Dan Koe)
    4. "We are the [third way]" — client's reframe
    5. "[N] [units] [proof metric]" — volume proof
    6. "[Customer logo] uses us to [outcome]" — social proof
    7. "Find your [magic action]" — CTA
    That's 7 slides.
