From AGENCIES (10 viral pieces analyzed):
  - HOOK 1: "[Big brands] are not smarter. They just have [X you don't]." — AdMakeAI template
  - HOOK 2: "[Expensive thing] was too [expensive], so I built my own." — Ikiform 190pts
  - HOOK 4: "Taste + Agency" meme — Dan Koe / Chimero / Greenhouse lingua franca
  - HOOK 5: "We're not replacing [X], we're reframing it." — 11x/Sitefire
  - Magic-action CTAs: "Find my creators" / "Generate my first ad" — beats "Sign up" 3-5x
  - LIVE counter pattern: "4,740 ads generated this month"
  - Two-tier CTA: free entry + magic-action
  - WINNING VISUAL: monochrome + warm accent (orange/amber) OR green "we're not purple" signal
  - ANTI-PATTERN: no aurora, no glass card, no sparkle, no "excited to announce", no "we replace"

From POSTFUNDING (top 1 = Performative-UI satire, 1178pts/213cm):
  - The rebrand that goes viral has BOTH product story AND brand story
  - "For the [new era]" is dominant 2026 tagline frame
  - Color drift: navy + warm accent OR green (away from aurora/purple)
  - "We will not use [cliche]" public commitments page — what we WON'T do
  - ANTI-PATTERN: aurora blobs, glass cards, sparkle, mock IDE, "trusted by" marquee, middle-tier glow

From PQC (top 1 = Filippo Valsorda 551pts/248cm, "practitioner perspective" beats FUD 3-10x):
  - WINNING FORMULA: [Authority actor] + [Specific year] + [Imperative/roadmap verb]
  - "Generally available" / "Migration framework" / "FIPS 203 compliant" — trust phrases
  - "By [year]" specificity — Google 2029, Cloudflare 2029, borrow calendar
  - "Even [unexpected actor] is doing it" — your enemy is ahead
  - WINNING VISUAL: dark navy + lattice + monospace accent (8/10 of viral)
  - ANTI-PATTERN: FUD headlines ratio'd, jargon-heavy copy, QKD marketing out-of-favor, repackaged old content
