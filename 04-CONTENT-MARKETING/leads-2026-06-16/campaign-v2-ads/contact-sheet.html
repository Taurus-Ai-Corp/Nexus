WHAT CHANGED FROM v1 (the 7 fixes)

  • Copy scaffold: HOOK → PROBLEM → DEMO → PROOF → CTA (the gap the Anil repo doesn't fill — biggest miss in v1)
  • 6 ad-format reference frameworks: each ad picks ONE format and stays in it (Unboxing, Before/After, Tutorial, Product Hero, UGC, Testimonial)
  • Named avatar + product binding: @image1 = product/screenshot, @image2 = on-camera person. Real people showing real products.
  • Camera/lens compiler: every prompt ends with "shot on a full-frame digital cinema camera, warm-toned cinema prime at 50mm, f/1.4, shallow depth of field, 8K"
  • Plain-English framing: no "Taste + Agency" / "agentic web" / "we are the agency we wish existed." Replaced with "I just got my brand back from a $40,000 agency" etc.
  • Specific proof metrics: every ad has a number, a timeframe, or a customer name (147 brands / 1,400 leads / 4,200 followers / 89 solo founders)
  • Normal-human hooks: first line is a real human problem, not a reframe
