# Campaign v2 — Anil-Matcha Toolbox Applied
**Built:** 2026-06-16 (replaces v1)
**What changed:** The 6 v1 ads were abstract editorial photos with founder Twitter copy. v2 uses the Open-Generative-AI toolbox: 6 ad-format reference frameworks, named avatar slots, product binding, camera compiler, and a real 5-beat copy scaffold.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
THE 7 FIXES APPLIED (what's different from v1)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. **COPY SCAFFOLD: HOOK → PROBLEM → DEMO → PROOF → CTA** (the gap the Anil repo doesn't fill, biggest miss in v1)
2. **6 AD-FORMAT REFERENCE FRAMEWORKS** — UGC, Tutorial, Unboxing, Before/After, Testimonial, Product Hero. Each ad now picks ONE format and stays in it.
3. **NAMED AVATAR + PRODUCT BINDING** — @image1 = product/screenshot, @image2 = on-camera person. Real people showing real products, not abstract editorial photos.
4. **CAMERA/LENS PROMPT COMPILER** — every prompt now ends with a compiled phrase: "shot on a full-frame digital cinema camera, using a warm-toned cinema prime lens at 50mm, aperture f/1.4, shallow depth of field, creamy bokeh, 8K resolution"
5. **PLAIN-ENGLISH FRAMING** — copy now uses words a normal buyer understands. No "Taste + Agency" / "agentic web" / "we are the agency we wish existed." Those got ratio'd for a reason.
6. **SPECIFIC PROOF METRICS** — every ad has a number, a timeframe, or a customer name. "147 last quarter" beats "we ship a lot."
7. **NORMAL-HUMAN HOOKS** — first line is a real human problem, not a reframe. "Your ad is invisible on the agentic web" → "Nobody sees your ads anymore because they're on AI search now."
