### LEAD PIPELINE STAGES

**Stage 1: PROSPECT** 
- Status: Researched and qualified
- Actions: Initial research completed, contact info found
- Next: Send initial outreach email
- SLA: Contact within 24 hours of adding to CRM

**Stage 2: CONTACTED**
- Status: Initial outreach sent
- Actions: Email sent, LinkedIn connection request
- Next: Follow-up sequence activated
- SLA: Follow-up if no response within 3 days

**Stage 3: ENGAGED**
- Status: Responded to outreach
- Actions: Meeting scheduled or email conversation started
- Next: Discovery meeting or detailed discussion
- SLA: Schedule meeting within 48 hours

**Stage 4: MEETING SCHEDULED**
- Status: Discovery meeting booked
- Actions: Meeting confirmed, audit prepared
- Next: Conduct discovery meeting
- SLA: Send meeting agenda 24 hours before

**Stage 5: MEETING COMPLETED**
- Status: Discovery meeting held
- Actions: Needs assessment complete, proposal required
- Next: Prepare and send custom proposal
- SLA: Send proposal within 48 hours of meeting

**Stage 6: PROPOSAL SENT**
- Status: Custom proposal delivered
- Actions: Proposal presented or emailed
- Next: Follow-up and negotiation
- SLA: Follow-up within 5 business days

**Stage 7: NEGOTIATION**
- Status: Price or terms discussion
- Actions: Addressing objections, revising proposal
- Next: Close deal or return to nurture
- SLA: Respond to questions within 24 hours

**Stage 8: CLOSED WON** 🎉
- Status: Contract signed, client onboarded
- Actions: Welcome sequence, project kickoff
- Next: Client success management
- SLA: Kickoff within 7 days of signing

**Stage 9: CLOSED LOST**
- Status: Deal lost but relationship maintained
- Actions: Add to nurture campaign, future follow-up
- Next: Monthly valuable content, future opportunities
- SLA: Add to nurture within 24 hours

### CRM AUTOMATION WORKFLOWS

**Trigger:** New contact added to CRM
**Actions:**
1. Assign to sales rep (you initially)
2. Send welcome email template
3. Create follow-up task for 3 days
4. Add to appropriate email nurture sequence
5. Set reminder for LinkedIn connection

### WORKFLOW 2: EMAIL ENGAGEMENT TRACKING
**Trigger:** Email opened but not replied
**Actions:**
1. Wait 3 days
2. Send follow-up email #1 (case study)
3. If still no reply, wait 4 days
4. Send follow-up email #2 (free audit offer)
5. If no reply, add to nurture campaign

### WORKFLOW 3: MEETING BOOKED AUTOMATION
**Trigger:** Meeting scheduled
**Actions:**
1. Send calendar confirmation
2. Create audit preparation task
3. Send meeting agenda 24 hours before
4. Send meeting reminder 2 hours before
5. Create post-meeting follow-up task

### WORKFLOW 4: PROPOSAL FOLLOW-UP
**Trigger:** Proposal sent
**Actions:**
1. Wait 2 days, send "Did you receive the proposal?" email
2. Wait 5 days, send value reinforcement email
3. Wait 7 days, create phone call task
4. If no response after 14 days, move to nurture
