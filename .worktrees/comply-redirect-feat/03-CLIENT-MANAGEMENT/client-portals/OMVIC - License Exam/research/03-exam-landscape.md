## Part 1 — OMVIC Deep Dive

### 1.1 Regulator + product

The **Ontario Motor Vehicle Industry Council (OMVIC)** is the delegated administrative authority enforcing the Motor Vehicle Dealers Act, 2002. Any individual who sells cars in Ontario — salesperson or dealer-principal — must be registered with OMVIC, and registration requires successful completion of the **Automotive Certification Course (ACC)**, delivered exclusively by **Georgian College's Automotive Business School of Canada (ABSC)** on behalf of OMVIC. (Note: "AutoTrain Inc." was a brand/platform under Georgian College — Georgian College is the current and sole approved provider. Source: [omvic.ca/selling/education/automotive-certification-course](https://www.omvic.ca/selling/education/automotive-certification-course/), [georgiancollege.ca/omvic](https://www.georgiancollege.ca/omvic/).)

### 1.2 Exam mechanics (as of 2026)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Question count | **60 multiple-choice** |
| Time limit | **90 minutes** |
| Passing score | **80%** (raised — effective January 1, 2026; prior historical threshold varied 60–75%) |
| Format | **Open book** — hard-copy student manual permitted |
| Retake policy | 4 attempts per academic term; then wait + re-enrol + re-pay ($286.08) |
| Registration validity | Must register with OMVIC within **2 years** of passing or re-take course |

Source: [Georgian College OMVIC page](https://www.georgiancollege.ca/omvic/).

### 1.3 Proctoring model

**Dual delivery** — Georgian College offers both:

1. **Online proctored** — webcam + secondary smartphone camera + **Safe Exam Browser** software. (Georgian College runs this in-house — *not* ProctorU, Examity, or PSI. The secondary-phone rig is the distinctive signal.)
2. **In-person** — authorized testing sites across Ontario.

### 1.4 Course format

- **Self-study correspondence** — UPS-couriered textbook + optional online resources (publicly accessible without login).
- Student has up to one academic term to complete.
- Designation on pass: **C.A.L.E.** (Certified in Automotive Law and Ethics).

### 1.5 Cost stack

| Line item | Domestic | International |
|-----------|----------|---------------|
| ACC course + exam | **$286.08** | $561.08 |
| MVDA Key Elements refresher (existing licensees) | $202.86 | — |
| OMVIC new salesperson registration | ~$325 (estimate, varies by fee schedule) | same |
| Annual renewal (salesperson) | ~$150–200 | same |
| **Typical all-in first-year outlay** | **$650–$700** | $900+ |

Source: [Georgian College OMVIC page](https://www.georgiancollege.ca/omvic/), [Examzify cost blog](https://omvicpractice.examzify.com/blog/how-much-does-an-omvic-ef280e77).

### 1.6 Registered population

- **~30,000 registered salespersons** in Ontario
- **~8,000 motor vehicle dealers** (businesses)
- Source: OMVIC 2023 Annual Report (most recent with public numbers).

### 1.7 Annual new-candidate intake (estimate)

OMVIC does not publish new-course enrolments directly. Reasoning:
- Active salesperson pool ~30,000; Ontario auto retail churn is high (~15–20% annually per industry norms).
- Implied annual new registrants: **4,500–6,000/year**.
- Plus 200–400 new dealer-principals (requires the course if not grandfathered).
- **Working estimate: 5,000–6,500 net new ACC exam candidates/year** (flagged as estimate).

### 1.8 Pass rate

- **Not officially published** by OMVIC or Georgian College.
- Forum sentiment (RedFlagDeals, GTAMotorcycle, YouTube) ranges from "ridiculously easy, blind monkey could pass" to "actually complicated."
- Given 80% threshold + open book + 4 attempts/term, first-time pass is likely **~70–80%**; eventual pass rate near 100%.
- Raised passing grade (60→80% in Jan 2026) will likely compress first-time pass rate 5–10 points.

### 1.9 Renewal + continuing education (MAJOR 2026 CHANGE)

**OMVIC's Continuing Professional Development (CPD) program launches April 1, 2026**, tied to license renewals from July 1, 2026:
- **Dealers: annual mandatory training**
- **Salespersons: every 2 years**
- **Grandfathered pre-2010 registrants:** must take Georgian College's "Key Elements" refresher
- 80% passing grade required; delivered online via OMVIC portal
- **Ontario is the only Canadian province with regulator-mandated CPD tied to renewal**.

This is a **massive TAM expansion event**: ~30,000 salespersons × every 2 years = **~15,000 CPD test-takers/year** from 2026 onward, on top of new registrants.

Source: [Canadian Auto Dealer, Sept 2025](https://canadianautodealer.ca/2025/09/omvic-rolling-out-mandatory-annual-dealer-training/).

### 1.10 Candidate pain points (scraped from forums)

- **Open-book ≠ easy** — common complaint is the manual is dense, poorly indexed; finding answers under 90-min clock is the real challenge.
- **Retake costs are painful** — failing means re-paying $286.08 + waiting for next term.
- **Dry, legalistic content** — Motor Vehicle Dealers Act, all-in pricing, disclosure rules, consumer protection. Perceived as rote memorization of regulatory minutiae.
- **Language barrier** — large share of GTA auto-sales candidates are newcomers; English comprehension of legal prose is a bottleneck.

### 1.11 Existing third-party prep (competitive landscape)

| Provider | Product | Price | Notes |
|----------|---------|-------|-------|
| **CourseTree Learning** (Toronto) | 400+ practice Qs, flashcards, tutorials | ~$99–199 | Claims 92% pass rate, money-back guarantee |
| **Examzify** | Practice tests, study guide | ~$29–79 | Multiple parallel SEO sites |
| **PracticeTestGeeks** | Free + paid practice | Free/$29 | High organic search rank |
| **YouTube creators** | Free review videos | Free | Informal, ad-supported |
| **Post-it method** (DIY) | Self-tabbing the manual | $0 | Most recommended in forums |

**Key insight:** No dominant branded prep player. All third-party offerings are generic question banks with thin content. **No AI-native, adaptive, Kerala/newcomer-language-aware platform exists.** Opening.

### 2.1 Big picture structural themes

- **Real Estate** (RECO): **110,000 registrants**, ~80,000 registration transactions/yr; largest population but already has mandatory Humber/Algonquin/Fleming programs. Prep market is highly fragmented (Passit dominates third-party).
- **LLQP**: 4 provinces share harmonized exam via CISRO. Advocis/PNC Learning/Olivers dominate. High volume, high turnover.
- **Securities (CSC)**: **Highest-volume single exam in Canada**. Wiley, CourseTree, AceCSE all compete. CSI is incumbent at $1,000+ price, massive margin opportunity for challenger.
- **HRPA**: Pass rate 55–60% — the **lowest** of any vertical surveyed. Largest pain point ⇒ highest willingness-to-pay for prep.
- **CPA**: Captive market via CPA PEP modules (can't avoid); third-party prep is supplementary (Densmore, PrepEngine). High spend per candidate ($2,500+).
- **CICC EPE**: Explosive growth with Canada's immigration consultant boom; only 4 sittings/year means concentrated demand spikes.

### 3.2 Recurring / renewal / CE TAM (the iceberg)

Beyond first-license prep:
- OMVIC CPD post-July 2026: 15,000 salespersons × $50–150 prep/renewal cycle = **$750K–2.25M/yr**
- RECO continuing education (24 hrs every 2 yrs across 110K registrants): $100–300/cycle = **$5.5M–16.5M/yr**

### 3.4 Cross-border US comparison (top-level)

US professional licensing prep is dramatically larger:
- **NASAA Series 7/63/65/66** (securities): ~100,000+/yr candidates, Kaplan/STC/Knopman $300–700 each = **$300M+/yr**
- **NMLS Mortgage**: ~100,000 loan officers/yr, OnCourse/Mortgage Educators $99–399 = **$40M+/yr**
- **Real estate** (state-by-state): 300,000+ new licensees/yr, $100–500 prep = **$60–150M/yr**
- **Life insurance** (state-by-state): 200,000+/yr, ExamFX/Kaplan $99–349 = **$40–70M/yr**
- **CFP Board**: ~10,000 CFP writers/yr, Dalton/Kaplan $600–2,500 = **$10–25M/yr**
- **US top 5 verticals alone = $450M–700M/yr** — roughly **6–8× Canadian TAM**.

## Part 4 — Strategic Implications for TAURUS AI

### 4.1 Why OMVIC is a strong beachhead

1. **Undefended** — no AI/adaptive prep; third parties are static Q banks.
2. **Concentrated regulator** — one exam, one syllabus, one delivery partner (Georgian) — easy to engineer content against.
3. **Newcomer-heavy candidate base** — GTA auto retail pool skews toward immigrants; language/legal-prose barrier is a real product moat.
4. **CPD launch July 2026** — triples the addressable pool (5–6K/yr → 20K+/yr) and adds recurring revenue.
5. **Low price ceiling is acceptable for MVP** — $99–199 AOV at 5K–20K candidates = **$500K–4M ARR** from OMVIC alone.

### 4.2 Expansion path

OMVIC → RECO (adjacent, Ontario, similar newcomer demographic) → LLQP → CSC.
Product moat = adaptive, language-aware, mobile-first, voice-enabled study.

### 4.3 Risk flags

- RECO prep is already commoditized (Passit dominant).
- CSC is a price-protected CSI monopoly; legal/trademark risk in content reuse.
- CPA is capture market — hard to penetrate.
- **OMVIC's 80% threshold + open-book paradox is the killer MVP thesis.**
