Frequently Asked Questions

Mater Maria offers four residence types to suit different lifestyles and budgets. The Lotus Studio (450 sqft) is ideal for independent seniors seeking a compact, well-appointed home. The Jasmine Suite (680 sqft) provides a spacious one-bedroom with separate living area. The Rose Villa (1000 sqft) is a two-bedroom premium villa for couples. And the Heritage Grand (1250 sqft) is our finest offering with two ensuite bedrooms, a study, and a private garden. All residences include barrier-free design, premium fittings, and access to all community amenities.
Yes, Mater Maria is inspired by Christian values and rooted in Kerala's Syro-Malabar Catholic tradition. Our community includes an on-campus chapel, daily prayer services, spiritual counselling, and faith-based programmes. However, we welcome residents of all faiths and backgrounds who share our values of dignity, compassion, and community. Our spiritual offerings are available to all but never imposed.
Healthcare at Mater Maria is comprehensive: 24/7 nursing staff and emergency response, daily health checkups and vital monitoring, doctor consultations twice a month, 24/7 ambulance service, hospital visit assistance, caregiver suites for those requiring dedicated support, post-surgical recovery packages, and our in-house Ayurvedic spa and wellness centre. We also maintain relationships with leading hospitals in Kottayam for specialist care.

Our community dining hall and outdoor areas are designed to accommodate family gatherings, and we host regular community events that families are invited to attend.
We understand the companionship pets provide. Small, well-behaved pets are permitted in select residences (Rose Villa and Heritage Grand) subject to community guidelines. Pet owners are expected to ensure their animals do not disturb other residents and comply with our pet policy regarding vaccinations, leashing in common areas, and waste management. Please discuss specific requirements with our team during your visit.
