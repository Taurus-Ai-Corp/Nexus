## Variant 1 — Legacy & Roots

```
Hook: Your next chapter, in Kerala.
CTA: Learn More
```

Strongest line: *"After two decades in the Gulf, the question is no longer if you return — it is where, and with what."* This earns the read because it names the precise unfinished sentence in the reader's head and refuses the false binary. The italic *if/where/with what* sequence reads as a financial advisor's cadence, not a developer's.

Weakest: the CTA. "Learn More" is the laziest button in the Meta library and undercuts a paragraph of restraint. The body has already promised arithmetic ("10% annual interest while construction proceeds"); the button should land that promise.

Rewrite (CTA only): **See the 15-year model** or **Read the offer document**. Headline mirror: *Reserve from Rs 5 lakhs* (already correct in production notes — pull it up to the button).

Score: 25/30 — SHIP, swap CTA before launch.

## Variant 2 — ROI-First Investor

```
Hook: 10% interest. Years 1-4.
CTA: Get Quote
```

Strongest line: *"The numbers, simply: Rs 5 lakhs to Rs 30 lakhs deposited as a hybrid share-and-deposit instrument."* The two-word opener "The numbers, simply" is the entire voice in three words — a 50-year-old chief accountant in Sharjah will read on. The instrument is named correctly (hybrid share-and-deposit), not euphemized.

Weakest: *"Add a guest house and event hall you can use rent-free."* This is the one line in the variant that breaks the prospectus voice — it reads like an upsell tossed into a financial summary. Move the perks to a separate line item or a creative slot 2/3 of the carousel.

Rewrite: replace that sentence with — *Resident perks include an on-site guest house and event hall, allotted annually.*

Score: 28/30 — SHIP. Highest-scoring variant in the set; lead with this in week-1 budget.

## Variant 3 — Faith & Community

```
Hook: Built on Bishop Pulickal's land.
CTA: Learn More
```

Strongest line: *"clergy who have walked alongside Kerala's diaspora for decades."* This is the line that does the trust work without pandering — it acknowledges shared history without invoking it as authority. The visual brief's instruction to NOT do a portrait close-up is correct and should be enforced.

Weakest: the hook itself. "Built on Bishop Pulickal's land" implies a transactional proximity to clergy — exactly the reading the voice brief wants to avoid. It also overweights the bishop as the trust signal in line one of line one.

Rewrite (hook): **A retirement built with the Pravasi Apostolate.** This keeps the institutional signal, surfaces the diaspora-care body that actually does the work, and reads as a fact rather than an endorsement.

Score: 25/30 — SHIP, with hook rewrite. Second budget priority for Christian Malayali NRI segment.

## Variant 4 — Healthcare Safety Net for Parents

```
Hook: Your parents. On-site nursing.
CTA: Learn More
```

Strongest line: *"Most NRI families have the same private fear: a 3 AM call from Kerala about a parent who has fallen."* This is the best opening sentence in the entire campaign. It is specific (3 AM, fallen), it is shared (the word *private* doing the work), and it does not flinch.

Weakest: the closing pivot to investment economics — *"share-based, from Rs 5 lakhs, with 10% annual interest"* — feels appended. The variant's emotional center is the 3 AM call; the financial pitch should be a footer line, not the body's final clause.

Rewrite (final two sentences): *The investor program lets you participate from Rs 5 lakhs without buying a full residence — full terms in the offer document.* Drop the "10% annual interest" repetition; it lives in V2.

Score: 26/30 — SHIP, tighten close. Strong scroll-stop, run as Reels by week 2.

## Variant 5 — Exclusive 90 Residences

```
Hook: Only 90. Already moving.
CTA: Apply Now
```

Strongest line: *"twenty Independent Villas, fifty Walk-up Villas, twenty Executive Apartments."* The arithmetic is the proof. This is the voice working — counted residences, not "exclusive collection".

Weakest: *"Most early commitments are coming from Dubai and Doha."* Without a verifiable number, this is the closest the campaign comes to fabricated social proof. The voice brief explicitly says "never fabricated scarcity" — this sentence currently sits on that line and the audience will sense it. Also: "Already moving" in the hook is a developer-brochure tic.

Rewrite (hook + social proof line): Hook becomes **Ninety residences. Four tiers.** Social proof line becomes *Reservations to date are concentrated in the GCC — see current allotment in the offer document.* This makes the claim verifiable and points to evidence rather than asserting momentum.

Score: 24/30 — ITERATE. Do not ship until hook is changed and the "already moving" line is replaced with verifiable language.

## Cross-Cutting Patterns

What worked across the set: the campaign disciplined itself on three voice habits — (1) named arithmetic instead of vague returns, (2) institution names spelled out (Pravasi Apostolate, MMT Hospital Annexure), and (3) one-clause concessions before the pitch ("This is not a holiday home", "The numbers, simply"). The italics-for-emphasis pattern is restrained and does the job a less disciplined writer would do with bolding or exclamation points. Cliche density is genuinely low — no "God's Own Country", no "embark on a journey", no emoji barrages. The voice brief landed in the work.

What failed across the set: three of five variants default to "Learn More" as the CTA, which is the Meta-default tell of a campaign that did not finish its own thinking. For an audience that has read prospectuses before, "Learn More" reads as a brochure request — replace with offer-document or calculator-specific verbs. Second pattern failure: the campaign over-relies on the bishop and clergy for trust signaling, with V3 hooking on it directly and V1's family-walking-the-orchard visual implicitly invoking it. One faith-anchored variant is correct; two is over-indexing on a single trust mechanism. Third: V5 strays into developer-brochure language ("already moving", "lock the year-one entry economics") which the rest of the campaign earned the right to avoid. The campaign as a whole over-relies on physical-place specificity (Elangulam, Ponkunnam-Pala valley) — strong, but at the expense of the people-led trust signals (Rajeev Abraham, Thomas Abraham, Paul Jose) which are entirely absent from the creative.

## Ship Recommendation (best to worst)

1. **V2 ROI-First Investor** (28) — leads with arithmetic, hits the chief-accountant target directly. Fund first.
2. **V4 Healthcare Safety Net** (26) — best scroll-stop hook in the set; emotional pivot is the campaign's sharpest weapon.
3. **V1 Legacy & Roots** (25) — clean and disciplined; pair with V2 for the rational-emotional A/B.
4. **V3 Faith & Community** (25) — ship after hook rewrite; reserve for Christian-Malayali interest segment, not broad audience.
5. **V5 Exclusive 90 Residences** (24) — hold from week-1 budget; ship in week 2 after rewrite of hook and social-proof line.
