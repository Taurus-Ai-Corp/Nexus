PRD

A practical product requirements document for the first revenue-generating version.
MVP scope

Product name

NeoVibe AI Platform — AI workflow automation for regulated financial services, document-heavy operations, and synthetic media production.

B2B SaaSAI agentsFintechLending

Core promise

Help teams turn messy inputs into structured decisions, customer acquisition actions, and polished sales/demo assets without building custom AI infrastructure.

Primary users

Founders, sales teams, fintech ops teams, lending analysts, document processors, and creative producers.

Target customers

UAE ADGM/DFSA fintechs, India micro-business lenders, SMB finance teams, and businesses needing visual damage previews.

Buying trigger

Manual research, document review, underwriting notes, outreach, and visual content creation are slow, inconsistent, or expensive.

Problem statement

Companies have valuable documents, prospect lists, loan files, and visual content needs, but most teams still rely on manual copy-paste workflows, inconsistent analyst judgment, and expensive creative production. They need an AI workflow layer that is structured, reviewable, and connected to revenue actions.

MVP modules

ModuleInputOutputRevenue use
Revenue LaunchpadMarket filters, target sectorsProspect list, outreach drafts, follow-up tasksUAE/India pilot acquisition

Deck + Video BuilderPRD, demo plan, scriptSlide outline, storyboard, motion notesSales and investor storytelling

Non-goals for MVP

  • No production lending decision engine.
  • No automated regulated approval decisions.
  • No full video rendering pipeline yet.
  • No customer data training or fine-tuning in MVP.
  • No replacement for human review.
Human-in-the-loop: every AI output should be reviewable, editable, and exportable.

Success metrics

Time saved per workflow, number of qualified prospects, pilot conversion rate, loan file triage speed, invoice extraction accuracy, and paid conversion after free pilot.

Pricing hypothesis

$1,999/month for managed pilot, or $0.15 per loan/document processed. Enterprise custom pricing for workflow integrations.

MVP acceptance

Customer can upload/select input, receive structured AI output, edit it, export it, and trigger a follow-up workflow or demo asset.
