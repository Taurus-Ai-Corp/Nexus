## 3. Corporate Endorsement Rules

### Tier 1: Main Product Brands
**Format:** "BrandName by Taurus AI"
**Usage:** Primary marketing, landing pages, app headers, login screens
**Example:** "Nexus by Taurus AI" — Nexus primary brand, Taurus AI parent visible

### Tier 2: Sub-Products (GRIDERA pattern)
**Format:** "BrandName | ProductName" (pipe separator)
**Usage:** Product-specific landing pages, within product UI
**Example:** "GRIDERA | Comply" — sub-brand of GRIDERA family
**Footer:** "A Taurus AI product" or "Powered by Taurus AI" (subtle)

### Tier 3: Watermark Only
**Format:** Small Taurus AI logo in footer
**Usage:** Enterprise clients, white-label scenarios, minimal corporate presence
