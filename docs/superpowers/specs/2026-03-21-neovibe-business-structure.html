CRITICAL: PE Risk Mitigation

Praveen must NEVER sign contracts, set final pricing, collect payments, issue invoices, or bind FZCO to any agreement. All contracts and invoices flow through the UAE entity. Violation creates a Permanent Establishment in India, making profits taxable at 25-30%.

Required Legal Documents

  • Employment Offer Letter (title, salary, variable commission, start date)
  • Employment Contract (IFZA standard template + variable comp addendum)
  • Refundable Advance Agreement (AED 6,790 deposit terms)
  • Profit Participation Agreement (separate from employment, NOT equity)
  • NDA + IP Assignment Agreement (all work product belongs to FZCO)
  • Non-Compete + Non-Solicitation Clause (12 months, UAE + India)
