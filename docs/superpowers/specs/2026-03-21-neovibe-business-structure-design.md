## 1. Executive Summary

This document defines the complete business structure for **Nexus by Taurus AI** — an AI-powered digital marketing enterprise operating under **TAURUS AI Corp.** (IFZA Dubai Silicon Oasis). Nexus targets Kerala/India SMEs with enterprise-grade digital marketing at 90% lower cost than traditional agencies, leveraging multi-agent AI automation for delivery.

### Key Decisions

| Decision | Choice | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| Entity structure | Trade name under existing FZCO | Zero additional licensing cost, activities already covered |
| First hire | Praveen Varkey — BDM on UAE employee visa | Kerala market access + client-facing role |
| Hiring model | Sweat Equity + Subsistence (AED 3,000 base + 25% commission + profit share) | Aligned incentives, self-sustaining by month 3 |
| Visa cost | Refundable Commitment Deposit (Praveen fronts ~AED 6,790) | Proves commitment, reduces founder's cash outlay |
| Tax strategy | Standard CT (NOT QFZP) — 0% on first AED 375K profit | Digital marketing is not a qualifying QFZP activity |
| Operating model | Lean Machine — 2 people + AI automation | $0 CRM, $0 PM tools, ~AED 5-7K/mo burn |
| Target market | Kerala MSMEs (23.79 lakh, 90%+ underserved) | Kottayam/Central Kerala is massively underserved |

## 4. Financial Structure

### 4.1 Tax Strategy

| Tax | Position | Action |
|-----|----------|--------|
| UAE Corporate Tax | Standard CT (NOT QFZP) — 0% on first AED 375K profit | File CT return via EmaraTax |
| UAE VAT | Not registered (below AED 375K threshold) | Monitor; register when/if turnover exceeds threshold |
| India withholding (TDS) | 0% — business profits under DTAA Article 7 (no PE in India) | Obtain UAE Tax Residency Certificate (TRC) annually |
| India GST (client side) | 18% IGST under Reverse Charge Mechanism — 100% recoverable as ITC | Educate clients during sales process |
| Canada tax | Report FZCO income on Canadian tax return; claim foreign tax credits | Consult Canadian CPA (Effin's tax residency status matters) |
