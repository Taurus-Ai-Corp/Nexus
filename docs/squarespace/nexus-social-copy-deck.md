## Before you start — two things the page editor cannot change

1. **The three products in Section 3 are real commerce Products.** Renaming them happens in
   **Products & Services → Shop**, not on the page. The page block only picks which products show.
2. **Nav labels are page names.** Renaming `Shop → Plans` happens in **Pages** (rename the page),
   which also changes its URL slug. Optional — see the last section.

## Known mismatches — worth knowing before you judge Squarespace on this

1. **The template is physical-goods commerce.** Cart, Add-to-cart and product variants are built
   for shipping objects, not SaaS subscriptions. Repurposing the products as plans reads fine
   visually, but real subscription billing needs Squarespace **Subscriptions** (Business plan or
   above) or an external processor.
2. **`Appointments` is the strongest genuine fit** — the free-audit booking is exactly what
   Squarespace Scheduling is for, and it is the one thing your Vercel site cannot do today.
3. **Brand deviation.** This site is orange `#FF5E1A`. The corporate Design System v2 assigns
   Nexus emitter `#4F7DF3` blue on void `#04060C`. Keeping orange was your call for this
   evaluation — just don't let it leak into production Nexus surfaces.
