Brand Architecture

Branded House

TAURUS AI is the only brand that carries equity. Platforms are descriptors: Taurus Gridera, Taurus Sentinel. One logo, one accent, product colour used only for wayfinding.

House of Brands

+Maximum M&A optionality

Rules & Open Questions

ALWAYS
Design the dark frame first; derive light from it.
One accent per screen. Red owns action.
Zero border-radius. Hairlines at 1px, never 2.
Mono for anything a machine produced.
Ship the static final frame with every animation.
NEVER
Gradient meshes or purple-to-blue SaaS washes.
Stock photography of people at laptops.
Emoji in product or corporate surfaces.
Red as a background fill larger than a button.
Rotating the mark, or recolouring individual blocks.
QUESTIONS WORTH ANSWERING NEXT
Should the attestation receipt become a public, embeddable badge? That is a distribution channel, not a feature.
Does the explorer get an agent-first view — identity as the primary object, transactions secondary?
Is DefQuan a platform, or the substrate all four run on? Naming it a platform may undersell it.
