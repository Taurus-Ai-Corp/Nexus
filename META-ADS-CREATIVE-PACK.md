## Image Specs for Designers

| Placement | Ratio | Min size | Safe zone | File |
|-----------|-------|----------|-----------|------|
| Feed single | 1:1 or 4:5 | 1080×1080 | Text in center 80% | Use existing PNGs, crop as needed |
| Stories/Reels | 9:16 | 1080×1920 | Keep text in center 60% | Add text overlay in Canva/Figma |
| Carousel | 1:1 | 1080×1080 per card | Consistent style | Reuse case study images |

**Design notes:**
- Use warm paper ground (#F6F4EF) and ink (#14110F) for text overlays
- Accent ochre (#B85C38) for CTAs
- DM Serif Display for headlines, Plus Jakarta Sans for body
- Keep text under 20% of image area for Meta approval
