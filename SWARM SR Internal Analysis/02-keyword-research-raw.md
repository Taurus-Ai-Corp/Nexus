# Nexus Platform - Geographic Keyword Research (UAE & India)

## Methodology Notes
- Sources: qualitative market research based on regional search behavior, agency buyer intent, and local business digital maturity.
- Search volume: no numeric volumes are claimed because live keyword tools/APIs were unavailable. Priorities are relative estimates based on commercial intent and fit with Nexus Platform capabilities.
- Intent classifications:
  - Informational (TOFU): researcher is learning or diagnosing a problem.
  - Comparison: researcher is evaluating vendors, tools, or approaches.
  - Transactional (BOFU): researcher intends to hire, buy, or request a quote.

## Priority Rationale
- **High:** Strong buying intent, direct service fit, high-value local business verticals (F&B, real estate, tourism, D2C, SaaS).
- **Medium:** Comparison or awareness-stage terms that require nurture but have good conversion potential.
- **Low:** Emerging/niche terms (PQC, scientific swarm) with longer sales cycles but strategic value for differentiation.
