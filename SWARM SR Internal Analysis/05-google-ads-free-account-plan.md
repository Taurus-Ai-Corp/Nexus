# Google Ads Free Account Setup + Keyword Plan for NEXUS Platform

## 1. What "Free Google Ads Account" Means
- You can create a Google Ads account at https://ads.google.com with **no mandatory spend**.
- The free tier gives you the **Keyword Planner**, ad creation tools, campaign drafts, billing paused until you add a payment method.
- You only pay when you launch a live campaign and set a daily budget.
- Google often offers new advertisers a small promotional credit (varies by country; check the signup flow).

## 2. Step-by-Step Free Account Setup

### Step 1: Create or use a Google account
- Use a Taurus AI Corp Google account (e.g. admin@taurusai.io).
- Go to https://ads.google.com and click "Start now".

### Step 2: Switch to Expert Mode immediately
- Google will push "Smart campaign" (automated).
- Click **"Switch to Expert Mode"** in the footer so you control keywords, match types, audiences, and geotargeting.

### Step 3: Create a "Search" campaign without going live
- Campaign goal: **Leads** or **Website traffic**.
- Campaign type: **Search**.
- Name it: `Nexus-UAE-Test-01` or `Nexus-India-Kerala-01`.
- When it asks for payment, **pause** or leave billing unconfigured.
- This still unlocks Keyword Planner.

### Step 4: Open Keyword Planner
- Tools & settings → Planning → Keyword Planner.
- Choose **"Discover new keywords"** and paste the keyword lists from `02-keyword-research-raw.md`.
- Set location to: United Arab Emirates or specific Indian states/cities.
- Set language to: English (and Arabic if targeting UAE Arabic searches).
- Download the keyword ideas CSV.

### Step 5: Build negative keyword list
- Add negatives early to avoid wasting budget:
  - `free`, `open source`, `template`, `internship`, `job`, `salary`, `career`
  - `how to become`, `course`, `certification`, `tutorial`, `learn`, `DIY`
  - Competitor brand names if you do not want to bid on them.

## 4. Match Type Strategy
- **Phrase match** for most keywords (controls intent, still broad enough).
- **Exact match** only on your highest-intent winners after 2 weeks of data.
- Avoid broad match alone at the start; it burns budget fast.

## 5. Landing Page Requirements
Each campaign needs its own focused landing page:
- Headline matching the keyword.
- One clear CTA ("Book a 15-min AI audit" or "Get a free content plan").
- Form: Name, Business name, Email, Phone, Monthly marketing budget.
- Trust signals: Taurus AI Corp FZCO license, Nexus brand, case-study placeholders.
- Geo-proof: local city/region references and currency (AED / ₹).

## 6. Conversion Tracking Setup
- Install **Google Tag** on nexus.taurusai.io.
- Create conversion actions:
  - Form submit (primary)
  - Phone call click (primary)
  - Page view of `/nexus/thank-you` (secondary)
- Verify the tag with Google Tag Assistant.

## 7. Budget Recommendation for First 30 Days
- Total test budget: **$600–$1,000 USD**.
- Split: 40% UAE local business, 30% India Bangalore/Mumbai, 20% Kerala, 10% UAE agents/blockchain.
- Goal: generate 15–30 qualified leads, not traffic.

## 8. Weekly Optimization Checklist
- Monday: Review search terms report; add irrelevant queries to negative keywords.
- Wednesday: Pause keywords with >50 clicks and 0 conversions after 7 days.
- Friday: Adjust bids by device and location if data shows winners.
- Sunday: Draft next week's ad copy variants using CL4R1T4S prompt patterns.
