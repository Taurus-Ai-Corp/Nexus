The core acquisition hook. Zero-risk entry for enterprise prospects.

## 4. Sales Enablement for Demo Calls

### Pre-Call Checklist
- [ ] Prospect's website, Instagram, and any recent campaigns reviewed
- [ ] Industry identified (fashion / real estate / F&B / other)
- [ ] Identify 1–2 likely "dead campaigns" to reference
- [ ] Prepare sample brief matching their vertical (use prompt lab if needed)
- [ ] Calendly booking confirmed, calendar invite sent with Zoom/Meet link
- [ ] Internal prep note: vertical, company size, likely budget tier

### Call Structure (15 Minutes)

| Min | Action |
|-----|--------|
| 0–2 | Introduce Nexus Creative in one sentence. Confirm: "You'll share your worst campaign, we'll show you the AI rebuild." |
| 2–5 | Ask: "What's a campaign from the last 90 days that underperformed?" Get the brief, product, and context. |
| 5–10 | Live demo: type their brief into the Prompt Lab. Generate output on screen. Walk through image prompt, headline, platform plan, deliverables. |
| 10–12 | Side-by-side comparison: original creative vs. AI-generated concept. Highlight specific improvements (headlines, color mood, platform fit). |
| 12–14 | ROI framing: "If this campaign had run with our output, the CTR uplift we typically see is 1.5–3x. At your current CPC of [X], that's [Y] saved or [Z] more conversions for the same spend." |
| 14–15 | Close: "The $99 proof-of-concept gives you a full campaign kit to test with your team. Or the Enterprise plan at $399/mo gives you unlimited. What fits your timeline?" |

### Objection Handling

| Obection | Response |
|----------|----------|
| "AI can't match our brand tone" | "That's exactly why we built Brand Memory & Tone Lock. The AI trains on your existing assets. We'll show you during the POC — if it doesn't match, you don't pay." |
| "We already have an agency" | "This isn't replacing your agency. It's giving them a 10x faster production tool. Most agencies we work with use Nexus for first-draft speed, then apply their human judgment on top." |
| "We need to see a longer track record" | "Fair. The dead campaign resurrection IS the track record. We use your own data, your own product, your own market. The comparison is your campaign vs. our output, not a case study from someone else." |
| "How do we know the images won't look AI-generated?" | "Nexus generates the brief and image prompt, not the final image. Your photographers or our pipeline (DALL·E 3, Flux, Imagen 3) produce the final asset. The prompt is engineered for editorial-grade output — not the plastic AI look." |
| "What about data security?" | "Enterprise plan includes SSO, dedicated infrastructure, and data isolation. Your brand assets never train our base model. SOC 2 roadmap available on request." |
| "This is too cheap for enterprise" | "The $99 POC is a risk-killer — you prove the value before committing. The $399/mo Enterprise plan is priced for scale, not scarcity. And the Custom tier gives you everything your procurement team needs: SLAs, API, dedicated tuning, white-glove onboarding." |
