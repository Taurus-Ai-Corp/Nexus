Why step 3 is blocked, not deferred

The research is explicit that the proof strip must be real client logos, a real press line, or nothing — never invented badges. I checked what we have:

  • Zero client logo assets exist anywhere in the repo.
  • All four case studies are anonymised — “Dubai F&B Brand”, “Kerala Retailer”, “Real-Estate Agency”, “UAE Pet Brand”. No named client, no quote, no attribution.
  • No press coverage, no awards.

A proof strip built from this would be decoration pretending to be evidence. That is the one move on the list that would actively cost us credibility rather than gain it, so I am not building it.

And one thing on the live site needs fixing regardless

case-studies.html is titled “Proof — Real outcomes from the NEXUS platform”. Underneath it, the first metric reads:

“Side-by-side demo showed projected CTR uplift of 2.3× and a 40% lower CPC.”

A projected number from a demo, presented under a heading that says real outcomes. The second card's “leads up 41% and CPL down 23%” carries no source either. That framing is a misrepresentation risk under Canadian consumer-protection law (Competition Act s.74.01), and it is the kind of thing a prospect checks.

Two honest fixes, both cheap: change the heading from “Real outcomes” to “Pilot results”, and label the projected figure as projected on the card itself. I have not made either change — copy claims are yours.

Already fixed

The third case study read “Real-Easte Agency” on the live page. Corrected to “Real-Estate”. Unambiguous typo, no judgement call, so I just fixed it.

The uncomfortable part of the verdict

Design review 01 said the campaign page is “the standard the rest has to reach.” The research says that was half right, and I want the correction on the record:

Real photography is table stakes in this set, not differentiation. A serif headline over a photograph with developer-marketing body copy beneath will read as a costume — the pixels are already there, the sequence is the gap.

So copying the campaign page's style to the other four verticals is the easy half and will not, on its own, move us out of mid-tier. The section order is the lever. That is why I stopped and built this instead of restyling four more pages.

What I need from you

  • Approve the proposed order (column three) and I'll apply it to the homepage first, screenshot it, and show you before touching the other pages.
  • The copy fix on case-studies.html — “Real outcomes” → “Pilot results”, and label the 2.3× as projected. Your call, it's your claim.
  • Proof: is there any client who would let us use their name or a quote? One named client changes what we can build more than any amount of layout work.
  • Footer: can I use the Windsor address from the corporate entity, and do you want routed mailboxes (hello@ / press@ / careers@)?
