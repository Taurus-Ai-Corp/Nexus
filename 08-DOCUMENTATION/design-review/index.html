You asked what the difference is. Here it is measured, not described — screenshots from a real browser at the same viewport, and counts pulled from the source.

Look at them next to each other

The nav item labelled “Campaign” is Nexus Creative. That page's own brand lockup reads Nexus Creative and its eyebrow reads AI Campaign Studio — while a separate /creative/ landing page also exists, with a different nav, different type scale and no imagery. Two pages, one product, two identities.

The image pipeline already exists

Five campaign images sit in platform/assets/campaigns/, each with a .json sidecar carrying the original prompt, an enhanced prompt, and the provider (vertex-imagen-3). This is a working, reproducible pipeline — not stock art. Only three of the five are used anywhere.

What I think we do

Direction only — nothing is built yet, and I am not starting until you pick.

  1. Promote the campaign page's language to the whole site. Raise the hero ceiling to 6rem, adopt the floating pill nav everywhere, and give every vertical a real photograph above the fold. That is the change that makes the site stop reading as a wireframe.
  2. Collapse the Campaign / Creative duplication. One product, one page. /creative/ becomes the marketing page; the current campaign build becomes the live demo it links to.
  3. Give every sub-product two buttons — one to read, one to try. A pilot/demo link per vertical, exactly as you described.
  4. Commission the missing photography through the same prompt pipeline. Two images already exist unused; Social, Intel, Freelance and Flow each need one.

Before I build — three things only you can answer

  • Nav: floating pill everywhere, or keep the flat bar on the corporate pages and pill on product pages?
  • Campaign vs Creative: merge into one page, or keep the campaign build as a separate linked demo?
  • Honest question back to you: is the compass photograph the register you want for all verticals? It reads editorial-fashion. Nexus Intel is a data product — the same treatment may fight it.
