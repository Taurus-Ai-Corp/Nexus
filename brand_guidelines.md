# Brand Guidelines: Taurus AI Corp

## 1. Brand Identity & Nomenclature
Taurus AI Corp sits at the intersection of deep tech, post-quantum cryptography, and autonomous AI agents. The brand reflects extreme competence, security, and developer-first transparency.
- **Nomenclature Rules:**
  - Always use **"NEXUS by Taurus Ai"** and **"Gridera by Taurus Ai"** in full on first mention.
  - **NEOFLOW™** must always include the trademark symbol.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Primary Background:** Deep Obsidian (#0A0A0A)
- **Surface/Card Backgrounds:** Dark Anthracite (#141414)
- **Primary Accent (NEOFLOW™ / NEXUS):** Neon Quantum Green (#00E676)
- **Secondary Accent (Gridera / Hedera):** Deep Purple (#6200EA) - *Used heavily for Quantum security workflows*
- **Text (Primary):** Almost White (#FAFAFA)
- **Text (Secondary):** Cool Gray (#A1A1AA)

## 3. Typography
- **Headings & Body Copy:** `Inter`, `Geist Sans`, or `Outfit`. Used for marketing copy, hero statements, and general readability.
- **Code & Technical Data:** `Geist Mono`, `Fira Code`, or `JetBrains Mono`. Used for all code blocks, CLI snippets, and metric readouts.

## 4. Visual Elements
- **Imagery:** Avoid abstract, human-vector illustrations. Use high-fidelity UI mockups (e.g., Nexus_Social dashboard), dark-themed terminal windows, architecture diagrams, and subtle glassmorphism effects.
- **Animations:** Micro-animations on hover (scale, glow). Avoid heavy parallax. Prioritize View Transitions for seamless page routing.
- **Iconography:** Sharp, minimalist, monoline SVG icons.

## 5. Tone of Voice
- Professional, technical, authoritative, yet approachable.
- Avoid marketing fluff. Use active voice and concrete metrics.
- Speak directly to developers: "Initialize your Gridera pipeline" instead of "Get started with our easy software".
