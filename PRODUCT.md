# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Dubai creative agencies, real estate marketers, F&B brand managers, and SMB owners who need campaign content, ad copy, and visual direction without hiring a full agency. They are time-constrained, visually literate, and judge quality in the first 2 seconds.

## Product Purpose

Nexus Creative turns a brief into a finished campaign concept — image direction, copy, platform plan, and deliverables — in minutes instead of days. It is a revenue-facing brand surface that proves the NEXUS platform can ship creative work at agency quality with AI velocity.

## Brand Personality

Editorial. Precise. Effortless.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS template with icon + heading + text cards
- Dark-tech AI look with purple glow and floating blobs
- Emoji feature grids and startup illustrations
- Cheap Canva-style output or templated stock imagery
- Loud gradients as a substitute for hierarchy
- Overly long headlines that read like a press release

## Design Principles

- Practice what you preach: the page itself must look like a premium campaign studio.
- Show, don't tell: the first viewport should feel like a finished editorial layout, not a feature list.
- Expert confidence: restrained typography, generous whitespace, decisive hierarchy.
- Speed as craft: interactions feel instant but polished; no friction in the live prompt lab.
- Own the aesthetic: warm paper palette, sharp serif/sans pairing, cinematic image crops.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA as baseline
- Reduced-motion alternative for all scroll/entrance animations
- Sufficient contrast on all body text and interactive elements
